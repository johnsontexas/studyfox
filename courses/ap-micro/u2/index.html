Unit 2: Supply and Demand

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Microeconomics · Lesson 2 of 6 · 9 min read

  • Equilibrium is where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded. A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage; a price floor above it creates a surplus.
  • Price elasticity of demand = %ΔQd ÷ %ΔP. Elastic (greater than 1): many substitutes, luxuries. Inelastic (less than 1): necessities like insulin.
  • Total revenue test: if demand is elastic, cutting price raises revenue. If inelastic, raising price raises revenue.
  • Other elasticities: income elasticity (normal vs. inferior goods), cross-price elasticity (substitutes positive, complements negative).
  • Consumer and producer surplus: the triangles above price (under demand) and below price (above supply).
  • Taxes create deadweight loss. The more inelastic side of the market pays more of the tax.
When both supply and demand shift, one of price or quantity is indeterminate unless you know the sizes of the shifts.
