Unit 5: Factor Markets

AP Microeconomics · Lesson 5 of 6 · 7 min read

  • Demand for labor is derived demand: it depends on demand for the product.
  • Marginal revenue product (MRP) = marginal product × marginal revenue. Firms hire until MRP = MRC (marginal resource cost).
  • In a competitive labor market, the firm is a wage taker, so MRC = wage.
  • Monopsony: a single buyer of labor. MRC is above the supply curve, so it hires fewer workers and pays a lower wage than a competitive market.
  • Least-cost rule: MP of labor ÷ wage = MP of capital ÷ rental price.
Hiring

The 5th worker adds 10 units, each sold for 3 dollars, so MRP = 30 dollars. If the wage is 25 dollars, hire the worker. If the wage were 35 dollars, don't.

Check your understanding

A firm should hire workers until:

  1. Total product is maximized
  2. MRP equals MRC
  3. Wages are lowest
  4. Average product is highest
Show answer

Answer: B. Hiring is profitable as long as each worker adds at least as much revenue as cost.
