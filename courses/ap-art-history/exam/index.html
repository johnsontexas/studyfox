The exam and how to analyze a work

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Art History · Lesson 1 of 5 · 7 min read

The course covers 250 required works in 10 content areas. The exam: 80 multiple-choice questions (1 hour, 50%) and 6 free-response questions (2 hours, 50%), including two long essays and four short ones. Some questions use works you haven't studied.

Four lenses for any work

  • Form: materials, technique, composition, color, line, scale.
  • Function: what it was made to do (worship, political power, remembrance, daily use).
  • Content: what it shows: subject, iconography, symbols.
  • Context: who made it, for whom, when, where, and how it was received.
Always connect form to meaning: "The pyramid's massive, stable form expressed the permanence of the pharaoh's rule."
Tip For each required work, know the title, culture, approximate date, materials, and at least one form-and-context connection. Flashcards with the image on one side work well.
