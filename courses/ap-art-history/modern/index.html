Later Europe and Americas (1750 to 1980)

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Art History · Lesson 4 of 5 · 8 min read

  • Neoclassicism (David's Oath of the Horatii) used classical order to express civic virtue. Romanticism (Goya, Delacroix) emphasized emotion, nature, and the sublime.
  • Realism (Courbet) showed ordinary life; photography changed how artists thought about representation.
  • Impressionism (Monet) captured light and modern life; Post-Impressionism (Van Gogh, Cézanne) pushed toward expression and structure.
  • Modernism: Cubism (Picasso's Les Demoiselles d'Avignon), Dada (Duchamp's Fountain), Surrealism (Dalí), Abstract Expressionism (Pollock), and Pop Art (Warhol).
  • Architecture: new materials like iron and steel led to skyscrapers and modernist buildings (Wright's Fallingwater).
