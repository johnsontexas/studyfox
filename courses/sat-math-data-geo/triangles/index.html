Lines, angles, and triangles

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Math: Data, Geometry, and Trig · Lesson 6 of 7 · 7 min read

  • Angles in a triangle sum to $180^\circ$. Angles on a straight line sum to $180^\circ$.
  • When a line crosses two parallel lines, corresponding and alternate interior angles are equal.
  • Similar triangles have equal angles and proportional sides. A line parallel to one side of a triangle creates a smaller similar triangle.
  • An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two far interior angles.
Example

Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are similar with $AB = 6$, $DE = 9$, and $BC = 8$. Find $EF$.

The scale factor is $\frac{9}{6} = 1.5$, so $EF = 8 \times 1.5 = 12$.

Check your understanding

Two angles of a triangle measure $48^\circ$ and $77^\circ$. What is the third angle?

  1. $45^\circ$
  2. $55^\circ$
  3. $65^\circ$
  4. $125^\circ$
Show answer

Answer: B. $180 - 48 - 77 = 55$.
