Probability and two-way tables

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Math: Data, Geometry, and Trig · Lesson 4 of 7 · 6 min read

Probability is $\frac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$. On the SAT, most probability questions use a two-way table.

Read the condition carefully. "Given that the student is a junior" means the denominator is the number of juniors, not the whole table.
Plays sportNo sportTotal
Juniors302050
Seniors254570
Total5565120

Probability that a randomly chosen student plays a sport: $\frac{55}{120}$. Probability that a junior plays a sport: $\frac{30}{50} = 0.6$. Probability that a sport player is a junior: $\frac{30}{55}$.

Check your understanding

Using the table, what is the probability that a randomly chosen senior does not play a sport?

  1. $\frac{45}{120}$
  2. $\frac{45}{65}$
  3. $\frac{45}{70}$
  4. $\frac{25}{70}$
Show answer

Answer: C. The condition "senior" limits the group to 70 students, and 45 of them play no sport: $\frac{45}{70}$.
