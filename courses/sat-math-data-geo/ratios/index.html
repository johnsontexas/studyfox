Ratios, rates, and units

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Math: Data, Geometry, and Trig · Lesson 2 of 7 · 7 min read

Set up a proportion with matching units in the same positions, then cross-multiply.

Example

A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of sugar. How much flour goes with 5 cups of sugar?

$\frac{3}{2} = \frac{f}{5}$, so $f = 7.5$ cups.

Unit conversions

Multiply by fractions equal to 1 so the units you don't want cancel.

Example

Convert 72 kilometers per hour to meters per second.

$72 \cdot \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ km}} \cdot \frac{1 \text{ h}}{3600 \text{ s}} = 20$ meters per second.

For area, square the conversion: 1 square foot is $12^2 = 144$ square inches. For volume, cube it: 1 cubic foot is $12^3 = 1{,}728$ cubic inches.

Check your understanding

How many square inches are in 3 square feet?

  1. 36
  2. 108
  3. 432
  4. 1,296
Show answer

Answer: C. 1 square foot is 144 square inches, so 3 square feet is $3 \times 144 = 432$.
