Forces and Newton's laws

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Physics Essentials · Lesson 2 of 5 · 7 min read

  1. First law: an object keeps its velocity unless a net force acts on it (inertia).
  2. Second law: $F_{\text{net}} = ma$.
  3. Third law: forces come in equal and opposite pairs acting on different objects.

Weight is $W = mg$. Mass (kg) doesn't change with location; weight (N) does.

Always draw a free-body diagram: one dot for the object, an arrow for each force. Then add forces in each direction to get the net force.

Check your understanding

A net force of 20 N acts on a 4 kg box. What is its acceleration?

  1. $5\text{ m/s}^2$
  2. $16\text{ m/s}^2$
  3. $24\text{ m/s}^2$
  4. $80\text{ m/s}^2$
Show answer

Answer: A. $a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{20}{4} = 5\text{ m/s}^2$.
