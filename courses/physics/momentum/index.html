Momentum and collisions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Physics Essentials · Lesson 4 of 5 · 6 min read

  • Momentum: $p = mv$
  • Impulse: $F\Delta t = \Delta p$. This is why airbags work: a longer stopping time means a smaller force.
In any collision with no outside force, total momentum before equals total momentum after.

In an elastic collision, kinetic energy is also conserved. In an inelastic collision (objects stick together, for example), some kinetic energy is lost.

Check your understanding

A 2 kg cart at 3 m/s hits a 1 kg cart at rest, and they stick together. What is their speed afterward?

  1. $1$ m/s
  2. $1.5$ m/s
  3. $2$ m/s
  4. $3$ m/s
Show answer

Answer: C. Momentum before: $2(3) = 6$. After: $(2 + 1)v = 6$, so $v = 2$ m/s.
