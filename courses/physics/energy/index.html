Work and energy

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Physics Essentials · Lesson 3 of 5 · 7 min read

  • Kinetic energy: $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
  • Gravitational potential energy: $PE = mgh$
  • Work: $W = Fd\cos\theta$, the energy transferred by a force
  • Power: $P = \frac{W}{t}$, measured in watts
Conservation of energy: without friction, $KE + PE$ stays constant. Energy changes form but the total doesn't change.
Example

An object falls from 5 m. All the $PE$ becomes $KE$: $mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$, so $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{98} \approx 9.9$ m/s. The mass cancels.

Check your understanding

What is the kinetic energy of a 2 kg ball moving at 3 m/s?

  1. $3$ J
  2. $6$ J
  3. $9$ J
  4. $18$ J
Show answer

Answer: C. $KE = \frac{1}{2}(2)(3^2) = 9$ J.
