Circuits

  • Ohm's law: $V = IR$ (voltage = current × resistance)
  • Power: $P = IV$
  • Resistors in series add: $R = R_1 + R_2$. In parallel: $\frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$.

Waves

Wave speed = frequency × wavelength: $v = f\lambda$

Frequency is measured in hertz (cycles per second). For a given wave speed, higher frequency means shorter wavelength.

Check your understanding

A 12 V battery is connected to a 4 Ω resistor. What current flows?

  1. $3$ A
  2. $8$ A
  3. $16$ A
  4. $48$ A
Show answer

Answer: A. $I = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{12}{4} = 3$ A.
