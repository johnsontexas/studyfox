Unit 6: Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Physics 1 · Lesson 6 of 8 · 7 min read

  • Rotational kinetic energy: $K = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$. A rolling object has both translational and rotational kinetic energy.
  • When objects roll down a ramp, those with smaller rotational inertia (relative to $mr^2$) reach the bottom faster: a solid sphere beats a hollow hoop.
  • Angular momentum: $L = I\omega$. It is conserved when no net external torque acts.
  • A spinning skater pulls in her arms, decreasing I, so ω increases.
  • Orbits: satellites conserve angular momentum, moving fastest when closest to the planet.
