Unit 1: Kinematics

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Physics 1 · Lesson 1 of 8 · 8 min read

The exam: 40 multiple-choice questions (80 min, 50%) and 4 free-response questions (100 min, 50%). Calculators are allowed on the whole exam, and you get an equation sheet.

  • Displacement, velocity, acceleration are vectors. Speed and distance are scalars.
  • Graphs: the slope of x vs. t is velocity; the slope of v vs. t is acceleration; the area under v vs. t is displacement.
  • Constant acceleration equations: $v = v_0 + at$, $x = x_0 + v_0t + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0)$.
  • Projectiles: horizontal velocity is constant; vertical acceleration is $-g$ (about $9.8\text{ m/s}^2$). Treat the directions separately; time links them.
Example

A ball is thrown horizontally at 6 m/s from a 20 m cliff. Fall time: $20 = \frac{1}{2}(9.8)t^2$, so $t \approx 2.0$ s. Horizontal distance: $6 \times 2.0 = 12$ m.

Check your understanding

What does the area under a velocity vs. time graph represent?

  1. Acceleration
  2. Displacement
  3. Force
  4. Speed
Show answer

Answer: B. Velocity times time is displacement, so the area under v vs. t gives displacement.
