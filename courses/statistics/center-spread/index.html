Center and spread

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Statistics & Probability · Lesson 1 of 4 · 6 min read

  • Mean: sum divided by count. Median: middle value when sorted. Mode: most frequent.
  • Range: max minus min. IQR: $Q_3 - Q_1$, the spread of the middle half.
  • Standard deviation: roughly the typical distance from the mean.
Outliers pull the mean and standard deviation a lot, but barely move the median and IQR. For skewed data, report the median and IQR.

A common outlier rule: a value is an outlier if it's more than $1.5 \times \text{IQR}$ below $Q_1$ or above $Q_3$.

Check your understanding

Data: 2, 3, 3, 5, 100. Which is the better measure of a typical value?

  1. The mean, 22.6
  2. The median, 3
  3. The range, 98
  4. The maximum, 100
Show answer

Answer: B. The outlier 100 drags the mean far above most of the data. The median, 3, is resistant to it.
