Studying for science classes

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Learn Any Subject · Lesson 5 of 7 · 5 min read

  • Learn vocabulary first. Science is full of terms, and you can't understand the ideas without them. Flashcards work well here.
  • Draw it. Sketch processes (the cell cycle, the water cycle, a circuit) from memory.
  • For problems (chemistry and physics), practice like math: write knowns and unknowns, pick the equation, check the units.
  • Read graphs carefully: title, axes, units, then the trend.
Tip Units are a free check. If you're solving for a speed and your answer is in kilograms, something went wrong.
