Preparing for a test

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Learn Any Subject · Lesson 6 of 7 · 5 min read

  1. Start about a week out. Several short sessions beat one all-nighter.
  2. Make a practice test from old homework, quizzes, and the review sheet, and take it under real conditions.
  3. Study your mistakes, not the material you already know.
  4. Sleep. Your brain stores memories while you sleep, so pulling an all-nighter undoes your studying.
  5. After the test, look at what you missed and why. It improves the next test.
Practice testing is one of the most effective study methods ever measured. Test yourself before your teacher does.
