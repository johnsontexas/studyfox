Taking notes that help

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Learn Any Subject · Lesson 2 of 7 · 5 min read

  • Don't copy everything. Write main ideas and examples in your own words.
  • Cornell method: split the page. Notes go on the right, questions or keywords on the left, and a two-sentence summary at the bottom.
  • Review within 24 hours. Cover the right side and answer the questions on the left.
  • For math and science, write down worked examples step by step, not just the final answers.
Tip Handwriting forces you to summarize, which helps you process ideas. If you type, resist the urge to transcribe word for word.
