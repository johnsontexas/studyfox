Studying for English classes

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Learn Any Subject · Lesson 4 of 7 · 5 min read

  • Read actively: keep a pencil or sticky notes handy. Mark confusing parts, repeated images, and big moments.
  • Summarize each chapter in two or three sentences when you finish it.
  • Track characters and themes in a simple chart as you read.
  • Build vocabulary by making flashcards of new words with the sentence you found them in.
  • Practice writing short responses. Writing is a skill that improves only with reps and feedback.

See Writing Strong Essays for how to turn your notes into an essay.
