Beating procrastination

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Learn Any Subject · Lesson 7 of 7 · 5 min read

  • Make the first step tiny: "open the doc and write one sentence." Starting is the hardest part.
  • Use a timer: work 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break (the Pomodoro method).
  • Put your phone in another room. Even a silent phone on the desk pulls at your attention.
  • Plan your week on Sunday: list due dates, then break big projects into daily steps.
  • Study at the same time each day so it becomes a habit rather than a decision.
Tip Procrastination is usually about avoiding a bad feeling, not about laziness. Shrinking the task shrinks the feeling.
