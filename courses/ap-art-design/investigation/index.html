Building a sustained investigation

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Art and Design · Lesson 2 of 2 · 6 min read

  1. Start with a question you actually care about.
  2. Practice, experiment, revise: show sketches, failed attempts, and changes in direction. Growth is part of the score.
  3. Document as you go: photograph work in progress, and keep notes on what you tried and why.
  4. Connect the works: each piece should build on what you learned from the last.
Graders look for a clear relationship between your inquiry, your making, and your revisions. Show the thinking, not just the finished pieces.
