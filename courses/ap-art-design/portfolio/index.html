How the portfolio is scored

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Art and Design · Lesson 1 of 2 · 6 min read

There is no written exam. You submit a portfolio in one of three courses: 2-D Art and Design, 3-D Art and Design, or Drawing.

SectionWhat you submitWeight
Sustained Investigation15 images showing an inquiry-guided body of work, including practice, experimentation, and revision, with written responses60%
Selected Works5 works (physical or digital images) showing your skill with materials, processes, and ideas, with written descriptions40%
Tip Your inquiry question drives everything. "How can I show memory through layered, fading images?" gives you more to explore than "I will draw portraits."
