Unit 6: Open Economy: Trade and Finance

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Macroeconomics · Lesson 7 of 7 · 7 min read

  • Balance of payments: the current account (trade in goods and services, income) and the financial (capital) account. They must sum to zero.
  • Foreign exchange market: if demand for the dollar rises, the dollar appreciates.
  • What changes exchange rates: relative interest rates, relative inflation, tastes for foreign goods, and relative incomes.
  • Effects: a stronger dollar makes U.S. exports more expensive abroad and imports cheaper, so net exports fall.
  • Higher U.S. interest rates attract foreign financial investment, which increases demand for dollars and appreciates the dollar.
Chain

The Fed raises interest rates, so foreign investors want U.S. bonds. Demand for dollars rises and the dollar appreciates. U.S. exports become more expensive, so net exports fall.
