Unit 2: Economic Indicators and the Business Cycle

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Macroeconomics · Lesson 3 of 7 · 8 min read

  • GDP = C + I + G + (X − M): consumption, investment, government purchases, net exports. It counts only final goods and services produced inside the country this year. Transfer payments, used goods, and stocks are not counted.
  • Real vs. nominal GDP: real GDP adjusts for inflation.
  • Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ labor force. Types: frictional (between jobs), structural (skills mismatch), and cyclical (recessions). The natural rate = frictional + structural.
  • Inflation: measured by the CPI. Inflation hurts lenders and people on fixed incomes, and helps borrowers (if unexpected).
  • The business cycle: expansion, peak, recession, trough.
Unemployment rate

200 million adults, 150 million in the labor force, 9 million unemployed. Rate = 9 ÷ 150 = 6%. (Adults not looking for work are not in the labor force.)
