The exam and how to draw graphs

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Macroeconomics · Lesson 1 of 7 · 5 min read

The exam has 60 multiple-choice questions (70 min, 66%) and 3 free-response questions (60 min, 33%): one long question and two short ones.

  • Label everything: axes, curves, and equilibrium points. Unlabeled graphs lose points.
  • Show shifts with arrows and new labels (AD₁ to AD₂).
  • Explain the chain: "Because the Fed bought bonds, the money supply increased, the interest rate fell, investment rose, and AD shifted right."
Tip Memorize the core graphs: PPC, supply and demand, AD/AS, money market, loanable funds, Phillips curve, and foreign exchange.
