Waves, sound, and physical optics

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Physics 2 · Lesson 6 of 7 · 8 min read

  • Wave speed: $v = f\lambda$. Transverse waves (light, strings) vs. longitudinal waves (sound).
  • Standing waves: on a string fixed at both ends, $\lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}$. In pipes, open ends are antinodes and closed ends are nodes.
  • Doppler effect: a source moving toward you sounds higher in frequency.
  • Interference: constructive when path difference is a whole number of wavelengths; destructive at half-wavelengths.
  • Double slit: $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$. Bright fringes are spread farther apart for longer wavelengths or narrower slit spacing.
  • Thin films produce colors (soap bubbles) through interference between reflections.

Check your understanding

If a wave's frequency doubles while its speed stays the same, its wavelength:

  1. Doubles
  2. Halves
  3. Stays the same
  4. Quadruples
Show answer

Answer: B. From $v = f\lambda$, if v is fixed and f doubles, λ halves.
