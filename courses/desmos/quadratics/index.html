Quadratics and other functions

Desmos for the SAT · Lesson 5 of 7 · 5 min read

Graph the function, click it, and read off what you need:

  • Zeros / roots / x-intercepts: where it crosses the x-axis.
  • Vertex, maximum, minimum: the turning point.
  • y-intercept: where $x = 0$.
  • Value of $f(a)$: define f(x)=... once, then type f(4) on a new line and Desmos prints the value.
For "how many solutions" questions, count intersections with the x-axis (or with the other graph). Zero crossings means no real solutions.
Tip Defining f(x) is also great for composition questions: type f(g(2)).

Check your understanding

You define $f(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 3$ in Desmos. What is the minimum value of $f$?

  1. $-5$
  2. $2$
  3. $3$
  4. $-8$
Show answer

Answer: A. The vertex is at $x = \frac{8}{4} = 2$, and $f(2) = 8 - 16 + 3 = -5$. Clicking the curve's lowest point shows $(2, -5)$.
