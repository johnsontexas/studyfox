Test-day tricks and traps

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Desmos for the SAT · Lesson 7 of 7 · 5 min read

Tricks

  • Equivalent expressions: graph the original and each answer choice. The correct choice lies exactly on top of the original.
  • Inequalities: type them directly, like y>2x-1. Desmos shades the solution region, so you can see which point is inside it.
  • Checking choices: for "which point satisfies...," type the point as (3,4) and see if it sits in the shaded region or on the line.

Traps

  • Degrees vs. radians: Desmos starts in radians. For trig in degrees, open the wrench (graph settings) and choose Degrees.
  • Missing parentheses: 1/2x is not the same as 1/(2x). Check that the typed expression looks like the one on the page.
  • Reading the wrong coordinate: solutions to one-variable equations are x-values.
Desmos isn't always faster. If you can solve something in one or two mental steps, just do it, and save the calculator for anything messy.
