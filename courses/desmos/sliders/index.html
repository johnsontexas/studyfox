Sliders for "what value of k" questions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Desmos for the SAT · Lesson 4 of 7 · 6 min read

When an equation has an unknown constant like $k$, $a$, or $c$, Desmos offers to add a slider. Drag it and watch the graph change until it matches the condition in the question.

Classic uses

  • No solution: slide until the two lines are parallel.
  • Exactly one solution for a quadratic: slide until the parabola just touches the x-axis (or the line).
  • Passes through a point: slide until the graph hits it.

Sliders get you close fast. Then confirm with algebra or by typing the exact value in. For "no solution," the slopes must match and the intercepts must differ.

Tip Click the slider's endpoints to change its range, and set the step to 1 if the answer should be an integer.

Check your understanding

For what value of $k$ does the system $kx + 2y = 8$ and $3x + y = 5$ have no solution?

  1. $k = 3$
  2. $k = \frac{3}{2}$
  3. $k = 6$
  4. $k = -6$
Show answer

Answer: C. Solve for $y$: $y = 4 - \frac{k}{2}x$ and $y = 5 - 3x$. Parallel lines need equal slopes, so $\frac{k}{2} = 3$ and $k = 6$. The intercepts (4 and 5) differ, so the lines never meet.
