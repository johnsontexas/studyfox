Desmos for the SAT · Lesson 3 of 7 · 5 min read

Type both equations on separate lines. The solution to the system is the point where the graphs cross. Desmos accepts equations in any form, so you don't need to solve for $y$ first.

  • One solution: the lines cross once. Click the intersection.
  • No solution: the lines are parallel and never cross.
  • Infinitely many solutions: the second line lands exactly on top of the first.
If a question asks for $x + y$ or some other combination, find the intersection first, then do the arithmetic. Reread what the question asks for.

Check your understanding

The system $2x + 3y = 12$ and $x - y = 1$ is graphed in Desmos. What is the value of $x + y$?

  1. $2$
  2. $3$
  3. $5$
  4. $6$
Show answer

Answer: C. The lines intersect at $(3, 2)$, so $x + y = 5$. Check: $2(3) + 3(2) = 12$ and $3 - 2 = 1$.
