Scales, keys, and intervals

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Music Theory · Lesson 2 of 4 · 8 min read

  • Major scale pattern: W W H W W W H (W = whole step, H = half step).
  • Minor scales: natural minor (W H W W H W W), harmonic minor (raised 7th), and melodic minor (raised 6th and 7th going up).
  • Key signatures: the circle of fifths. Sharps add in order F C G D A E B; flats in order B E A D G C F. A relative minor shares its major key's signature and starts on scale degree 6.
  • Intervals have a size (2nd, 3rd...) and a quality: perfect (unisons, 4ths, 5ths, octaves), major or minor (2nds, 3rds, 6ths, 7ths), augmented, or diminished.
  • Inversion: intervals add to 9 when inverted (a 3rd becomes a 6th) and major becomes minor.
