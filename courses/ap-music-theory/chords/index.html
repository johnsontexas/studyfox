Chords and Roman numerals

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Music Theory · Lesson 3 of 4 · 8 min read

  • Triads: major, minor, diminished, augmented. Seventh chords add a 7th above the root (the dominant seventh, V7, is the most common).
  • Roman numerals in major: I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°. In minor (with raised 7th): i, ii°, III, iv, V, VI, vii°.
  • Inversions and figured bass: root position (5/3), first inversion (6), second inversion (6/4). For sevenths: 7, 6/5, 4/3, 4/2.
  • Functions: tonic (I), predominant (ii, IV), dominant (V, vii°). Progressions usually move tonic, predominant, dominant, tonic.
