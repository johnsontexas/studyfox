AP Music Theory · Lesson 1 of 4 · 5 min read

  • Multiple choice: 75 questions (80 min, 45%), with and without recorded music.
  • Free response (55%): 7 written questions (melodic and harmonic dictation, writing voice leading from figured bass and Roman numerals, and harmonizing a melody) plus 2 sight-singing questions you record.
Tip Ear training takes daily practice. Ten minutes a day of interval and chord identification beats an hour once a week.
