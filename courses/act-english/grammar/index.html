Agreement, verbs, pronouns, and modifiers

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT English · Lesson 3 of 6 · 9 min read

Subject-verb agreement

Cross out the phrases between the subject and the verb. "The box of old photographs contains." Words like each, every, either, neither, everyone are singular.

Verb tense

Match the tense of the rest of the paragraph unless a time word signals a change. Use "had" plus a past participle for an action that happened before another past action.

Pronouns

  • Match the number of the noun: the company... its, the rocks... them.
  • Use who for subjects and whom for objects (if you could answer "him," use whom).
  • Its is possessive; it's means "it is." Their, there, and they're follow the same logic.

Modifiers

IncorrectWalking to school, the rain soaked my backpack.
CorrectWalking to school, I got my backpack soaked by the rain.
An opening descriptive phrase must be followed right away by the person or thing it describes.

Check your understanding

"Each of the players ______ a new jersey." Which verb fits?

  1. have
  2. has
  3. are having
  4. were having
Show answer

Answer: B. "Each" is singular, so the verb is "has." "Of the players" doesn't change the subject.
