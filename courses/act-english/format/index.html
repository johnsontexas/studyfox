How ACT English works

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT English · Lesson 1 of 6 · 5 min read

ACT English gives you essays with underlined portions. For each one, you choose the best version, and choice A is usually NO CHANGE. Some questions ask about the essay as a whole: whether to add or delete a sentence, where a sentence belongs, or whether the essay met a goal.

On the enhanced ACT you have 35 minutes for 50 questions, about 42 seconds each.

Read the full sentence, not just the underlined part. Many errors only show up when you see what comes before and after.
Tip NO CHANGE is right about as often as any other letter. Don't avoid it, and don't pick it just because the sentence "sounds okay."
Tip When all four choices say the same thing, the shortest grammatically correct one is usually right.
