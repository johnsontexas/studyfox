Concise and precise wording

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT English · Lesson 4 of 6 · 6 min read

The ACT rewards writing that says the most with the fewest words, as long as nothing important is lost.

IncorrectThe annual festival, which happens every year, draws large crowds.
CorrectThe annual festival draws large crowds.

"Annual" already means "every year," so the clause is redundant.

Word choice

For "most precise word" questions, pick the word whose exact meaning fits the context and tone. Avoid slang in a formal essay and avoid words that are close in meaning but slightly off.

Tip When the choices differ only in length, check the shortest one first. If it is grammatically correct and keeps the meaning, it is almost always the answer.
Tip "OMIT the underlined portion" is often correct when the underlined part is redundant or off-topic.

Check your understanding

Which is the most concise correct version: "She returned back to the city where she was born."

  1. returned back to
  2. returned to
  3. returned back again to
  4. went and returned to
Show answer

Answer: B. "Returned" already means "went back," so "back" is redundant.
