Detail and inference questions

ACT Reading · Lesson 2 of 4 · 7 min read

Most ACT Reading questions are about details or reasonable inferences.

Detail questions

"According to the passage..." means the answer is stated directly. Find it, then pick the choice that restates it in different words.

Inference questions

"The passage suggests..." or "It can reasonably be inferred..." means the answer is not stated outright, but it must be strongly supported. ACT inferences are small steps, not big leaps.

IncorrectChoices with extreme words (always, never, completely, only) unless the passage uses them too.
IncorrectChoices that are true in real life but not mentioned in the passage.
IncorrectChoices that use words from the passage but mix up who did what.

Check your understanding

A question says, "It can reasonably be inferred that the narrator..." What kind of answer should you look for?

  1. A fact stated word for word
  2. A small conclusion strongly supported by the text
  3. A dramatic prediction about the future
  4. Your own opinion of the narrator
Show answer

Answer: B. Inferences on the ACT are small, well-supported steps beyond what the passage says outright.
