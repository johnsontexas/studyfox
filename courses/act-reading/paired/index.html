Paired passages

Some ACT Reading sets give two shorter passages on the same topic. Questions ask about Passage A, Passage B, and how they relate.

  1. Read Passage A and answer its questions.
  2. Read Passage B and answer its questions.
  3. Then answer the questions about both. This keeps the two authors' views from blending together.

Passages with data

The enhanced ACT can include a chart or table alongside a reading passage. Read the title and labels, then check the specific numbers the question asks about. The right answer must match both the passage and the data.

Tip For "both authors would agree" questions, a choice must be supported by both passages, not just one.
