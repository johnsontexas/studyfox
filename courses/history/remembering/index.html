Remembering people, dates, and events

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

How to Study History · Lesson 4 of 4 · 5 min read

  • Build a timeline for each unit. Seeing order and gaps helps cause and effect make sense.
  • Tell it as a story. Explaining a unit out loud from memory reveals what you don't actually know.
  • Make flashcards that ask why, not just when: "Why did the Articles of Confederation fail?"
  • Connect to what you know: link new events to earlier units or to today.
Know the approximate era of everything, and exact dates only for the biggest turning points.
