How to Study History · Lesson 3 of 4 · 7 min read

  1. Thesis with a line of reasoning: answer the prompt and give your categories. "The New Deal expanded the federal government's role through economic relief, new regulations, and social programs."
  2. Group evidence by those categories, not in the order you read it.
  3. Use documents as evidence, not summaries. Explain how each one supports your argument.
  4. Add outside evidence: a fact or example you know that isn't in the documents.
  5. Source some documents using HIPP to show why the author's perspective matters.
Tip Document-based questions (DBQs) reward the argument, not the number of documents you mention.

  1. One paragraph per document, in order
  2. By the categories in your thesis
