Pronouns and possessives

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Grammar Rules · Lesson 6 of 9 · 5 min read

A pronoun must match the noun it replaces. Singular nouns (including companies, organizations, and countries) take it/its.
IncorrectThe museum announced that they would extend their hours.
CorrectThe museum announced that it would extend its hours.

Apostrophes

  • its = belonging to it. it's = it is.
  • their = belonging to them. they're = they are. there = a place.
  • Singular possessive: the scientist's notes (one scientist).
  • Plural possessive: the scientists' notes (several scientists).
  • Plain plurals take no apostrophe: "The scientists published results."
Tip On possessive questions, ask two things: is it plural or singular, and does it own something? Then pick the form.

Check your understanding

Choose the correct option: "Both ______ findings were published in the same journal."

  1. researcher's
  2. researchers
  3. researchers'
  4. researcher's'
Show answer

Answer: C. "Both" means more than one researcher (plural), and the findings belong to them (possessive), so: researchers'.
