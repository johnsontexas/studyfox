Verb tense and form

SAT Grammar Rules · Lesson 7 of 9 · 4 min read

Match the tense to the time frame already set by the passage. Don't switch tenses without a reason.
IncorrectIn 1905, Einstein publishes four papers that changed physics.
CorrectIn 1905, Einstein published four papers that changed physics.

Other verb traps

  • Past participles after has/have/had: has written (not wrote), had begun (not began).
  • Parallel structure: items in a list or comparison use the same form. "She likes hiking, swimming, and to bike" should be "biking."
  • A sentence needs a main verb. "The scientist studying the samples" has no main verb, so it's a fragment.

Check your understanding

Choose the correct option: "Last year, the team ______ a new method for filtering water."

  1. develops
  2. will develop
  3. developed
  4. has been developing
Show answer

Answer: C. "Last year" sets a completed past time frame, so the simple past "developed" fits.
