Unit 3B: Tangent, inverse trig, and polar functions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Precalculus · Lesson 8 of 9 · 8 min read

  • $\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, period $\pi$, with vertical asymptotes where $\cos\theta = 0$.
  • Inverse trig: $\sin^{-1}$ and $\tan^{-1}$ return angles in $[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}]$; $\cos^{-1}$ returns angles in $[0, \pi]$.
  • Reciprocal functions: $\csc$, $\sec$, $\cot$.
  • Polar coordinates $(r, \theta)$: $x = r\cos\theta$, $y = r\sin\theta$, $r^2 = x^2 + y^2$.
  • Polar graphs: circles ($r = a\cos\theta$), roses ($r = a\sin(n\theta)$), and limaçons ($r = a + b\cos\theta$). When $r$ is increasing, the graph moves away from the origin.

Check your understanding

Convert the polar point $(2, \frac{\pi}{2})$ to rectangular coordinates.

  1. $(2, 0)$
  2. $(0, 2)$
  3. $(0, -2)$
  4. $(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{2})$
Show answer

Answer: B. $x = 2\cos\frac{\pi}{2} = 0$ and $y = 2\sin\frac{\pi}{2} = 2$.
