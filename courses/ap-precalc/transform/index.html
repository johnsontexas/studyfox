Unit 1C: Transformations and modeling

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Precalculus · Lesson 4 of 9 · 7 min read

  • $g(x) = af(b(x - h)) + k$: $a$ stretches vertically (and reflects if negative), $b$ compresses horizontally by a factor of $\frac{1}{b}$, $h$ shifts right, $k$ shifts up.
  • Modeling: choose a function type that matches the data's behavior (constant rate: linear; constant change in rate: quadratic; turning points: polynomial).
  • Piecewise functions model situations with different rules over different intervals.
  • State assumptions and limitations: a model may only be valid over a certain domain.
