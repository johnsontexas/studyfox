Unit 3A: Periodic and sinusoidal functions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Precalculus · Lesson 7 of 9 · 9 min read

  • Radians: $\pi$ radians $= 180°$. On the unit circle, a point at angle θ is $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$.
  • Sinusoidal functions: $f(\theta) = a\sin(b(\theta + c)) + d$. Amplitude $|a|$, period $\frac{2\pi}{|b|}$, phase shift $-c$, midline $y = d$.
  • Modeling: find the max and min. Amplitude = (max − min) ÷ 2; midline = (max + min) ÷ 2. Period from the time between peaks.
  • Identities: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$; sum and double-angle formulas.
Example

A Ferris wheel goes from 2 m to 42 m every 8 minutes. Amplitude 20, midline 22, period 8, so $b = \frac{2\pi}{8} = \frac{\pi}{4}$.

Check your understanding

What is the period of $f(x) = 3\sin(4x)$?

  1. $4$
  2. $\frac{\pi}{2}$
  3. $2\pi$
  4. $8\pi$
Show answer

Answer: B. Period $= \frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
