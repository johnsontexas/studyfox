Using quotes and evidence

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Writing Strong Essays · Lesson 3 of 6 · 5 min read

  • Introduce every quote. Never drop a quote in as its own sentence.
  • Keep quotes short. Quote only the words you'll analyze.
  • Cite it the way your teacher asks (MLA uses the author and page: (Fitzgerald 43)).
IncorrectGatsby is hopeful. "He stretched out his arms toward the dark water."
CorrectGatsby's hope is almost physical: he "stretched out his arms toward the dark water," reaching for a light he can't touch.

Answer: B. A dropped quote stands alone with no lead-in, so the reader doesn't know why it's there.
