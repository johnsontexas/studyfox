Revising and editing

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Writing Strong Essays · Lesson 6 of 6 · 5 min read

  1. Revise big things first: does every paragraph support the thesis? Try a reverse outline: write one line summarizing each paragraph and check the logic.
  2. Then sentences: cut filler words ("very," "really," "in order to"), use active voice, and vary sentence length.
  3. Then proofread: read aloud slowly. Your ear catches what your eyes skip.
IncorrectIt is important to note that the character was very sad.
CorrectThe character was devastated.
