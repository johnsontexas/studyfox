Unit 4: American Political Ideologies and Beliefs

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP U.S. Government and Politics · Lesson 5 of 6 · 7 min read

  • Political socialization: people form beliefs through family (the strongest influence), schools, peers, media, religion, and major events.
  • Core values: individualism, equality of opportunity, free enterprise, rule of law, and limited government.
  • Generational effects (shared events shape a cohort) and life-cycle effects (views change with age).
  • Polling: good polls use random samples, neutral wording, and report a margin of error. Watch for biased samples and leading questions.
  • Ideologies: liberals generally favor more government action on the economy and social programs; conservatives favor free markets and less economic regulation; libertarians favor minimal government in both economic and social life.
  • Economic policy: Keynesian (government spending to manage demand) vs. supply-side (tax cuts to boost production). The Federal Reserve sets monetary policy.
