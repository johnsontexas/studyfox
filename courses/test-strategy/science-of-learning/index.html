How learning actually works

SAT & ACT Study Strategy · Lesson 1 of 6 · 6 min read

Rereading notes and highlighting feel productive, but research on learning shows they're among the weakest methods. Four habits do most of the work:

1. Active recall

Pull information out of your memory instead of putting it in again. Close the book and try to write the formula or rule. Struggling to remember is what makes it stick.

2. Spaced repetition

Review something a day later, then a few days later, then a week later. Short sessions spread out beat one long cram session.

3. Interleaving

Once you know a few skills, mix them. Mixed sets force you to recognize which method a question needs, and that's exactly what the real test demands.

4. Learning from mistakes

Every missed question is a lesson. Figure out why you missed it before moving on.
