SAT & ACT Study Strategy · Lesson 4 of 6 · 4 min read

Grammar is the most learnable part of the SAT, because the same small set of rules is tested again and again.

  1. Learn the rules from the SAT Grammar Rules course.
  2. Drill one rule at a time until you can spot it instantly.
  3. Keep a rule log. For every miss, write the rule you broke in one line. Turn the log into a flashcard set.
  4. Mix all rules together once each is solid.
Tip Don't trust "sounds right." The SAT deliberately writes wrong answers that sound natural in casual speech. Name the rule before you pick.

For Transitions and Rhetorical Synthesis, focus on logic: what's the relationship between the two sentences, and what's the specific goal the question states?
