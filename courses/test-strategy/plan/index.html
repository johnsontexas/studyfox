Building a study plan

SAT & ACT Study Strategy · Lesson 6 of 6 · 5 min read

  1. Take a diagnostic. Start with a half-length practice test to see where you stand.
  2. Pick two or three weak areas. Your dashboard shows accuracy by domain.
  3. Study in short, regular sessions. 30 to 45 minutes, four or five days a week, beats one long weekend session.
  4. Split each session: a few minutes of flashcard review, then learning or drilling one weak skill, then a short mixed set.
  5. Review your mistakes weekly from the review log.
  6. Take a full practice test every 2 to 3 weeks to track progress and build stamina.
The week before the test: take one last full practice test, review mistakes, and rest. Get good sleep the last few nights.
