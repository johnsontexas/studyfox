SAT & ACT Study Strategy · Lesson 5 of 6 · 5 min read

ACT Science is mostly a data-reading test. You rarely need outside science knowledge.

How to approach a passage

  1. Skim the text quickly. Don't try to understand every detail.
  2. Go straight to the figures. Read the title, the axes, and the units.
  3. Look for trends: as one variable goes up, does the other go up, down, or stay flat?
  4. Answer from the data. Most questions point you to a specific table or figure.

Conflicting viewpoints

When two or more scientists disagree, write a one-line summary of each view. Most questions ask where they agree or differ.

Tip Practice reading graphs from news articles and textbooks. Speed at reading axes and units is the real skill.
