Learning math concepts

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT & ACT Study Strategy · Lesson 2 of 6 · 6 min read

Understand it, then practice it

  1. Learn the idea. Read the formula sheet section or an explanation, and ask why it works. A formula you understand is much easier to remember.
  2. Study one worked example step by step.
  3. Do 5 to 10 questions of just that skill in Tutor mode, starting at Easy and moving up.
  4. Mix it in with other skills once you're getting most right.

Write out your steps

Most math mistakes on the SAT are careless ones: a dropped negative sign or answering for $x$ when the question asked for $2x$. Writing each step, even briefly, catches them.

Sort your mistakes

  • Concept: you didn't know how to do it. Relearn the idea.
  • Careless: you knew it but slipped. Slow down on that step next time.
  • Time: you knew it but ran out of time. Practice with Speed mode or Desmos shortcuts.
Tip Explain a concept out loud as if teaching a friend. If you get stuck, that's the exact gap to fill.

Check your understanding

You missed a question because you solved for $x$ but the question asked for $x + 3$. What type of mistake is this?

  1. Concept
  2. Careless
  3. Time
  4. Vocabulary
Show answer

Answer: B. You knew how to solve it but didn't answer what was asked. The fix is a habit: underline what the question asks for and check before choosing.
