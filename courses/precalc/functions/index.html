Functions and transformations

Algebra 2 & Precalculus · Lesson 1 of 4 · 7 min read

A function gives exactly one output for each input. The domain is the set of allowed inputs, and the range is the set of outputs.

Transformations of $f(x)$

ChangeEffect
$f(x) + k$up $k$
$f(x - h)$right $h$ (the opposite of the sign)
$-f(x)$reflect over the x-axis
$f(-x)$reflect over the y-axis
$a \cdot f(x)$vertical stretch by $a$

Composition and inverses

$f(g(x))$ means: apply $g$ first, then $f$. To find an inverse, swap $x$ and $y$, then solve for $y$.

Check your understanding

How is the graph of $g(x) = (x + 3)^2 - 5$ related to $f(x) = x^2$?

  1. Right 3, down 5
  2. Left 3, down 5
  3. Left 3, up 5
  4. Right 3, up 5
Show answer

Answer: B. $x + 3 = x - (-3)$ shifts left 3, and $-5$ outside shifts down 5.
