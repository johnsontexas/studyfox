Polynomials and rational functions

Algebra 2 & Precalculus · Lesson 3 of 4 · 7 min read

Polynomials

  • End behavior depends on the degree and leading coefficient. Even degree: both ends point the same way. Odd degree: opposite ways.
  • A zero with odd multiplicity crosses the x-axis; a zero with even multiplicity touches and bounces.

Rational functions

Vertical asymptotes occur where the denominator is zero (after canceling common factors). A canceled factor makes a hole instead.

Horizontal asymptote, comparing degrees of the top and bottom:

  • Bottom degree bigger: $y = 0$.
  • Same degree: $y = $ ratio of leading coefficients.
  • Top degree bigger: no horizontal asymptote.

Check your understanding

What is the horizontal asymptote of $f(x) = \dfrac{2x + 1}{x - 3}$?

  1. $y = 0$
  2. $y = 2$
  3. $y = 3$
  4. $y = -\frac{1}{3}$
Show answer

Answer: B. The top and bottom have the same degree, so the asymptote is the ratio of leading coefficients: $\frac{2}{1} = 2$. (The vertical asymptote is $x = 3$.)
