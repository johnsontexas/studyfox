Exponentials and logarithms

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Algebra 2 & Precalculus · Lesson 2 of 4 · 7 min read

Exponential functions $f(x) = a \cdot b^x$ grow (if $b > 1$) or decay (if $0 < b < 1$) by the same factor each step.

A logarithm answers "what exponent?": $\log_b x = y$ means $b^y = x$.

Log rules

  • $\log_b(xy) = \log_b x + \log_b y$
  • $\log_b\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_b x - \log_b y$
  • $\log_b(x^n) = n \log_b x$
  • Change of base: $\log_b x = \dfrac{\ln x}{\ln b}$
Example

Solve $3^x = 20$. Take the log of both sides: $x \ln 3 = \ln 20$, so $x = \dfrac{\ln 20}{\ln 3} \approx 2.73$.
