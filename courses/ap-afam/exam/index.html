AP African American Studies · Lesson 1 of 5 · 5 min read

AP African American Studies is interdisciplinary: history, literature, art, music, political science, and geography. You'll study primary sources like speeches, poems, photographs, and maps.

  • Multiple choice: 60 questions in 70 minutes, most tied to a source (text or image). 60% of the score.
  • Free response: 4 questions in 80 minutes, including source analysis and an argument essay. 40% of the score.
  • Individual research project: part of the course, completed in class, but not part of the AP exam score.
Tip When reading a source, always ask: who made it, when, for whom, and why? Then connect it to the broader movement or period.
