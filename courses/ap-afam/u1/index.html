Unit 1: Origins of the African Diaspora

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP African American Studies · Lesson 2 of 5 · 7 min read

  • Africa's diversity: Africa is a huge continent with many geographic regions, languages, and cultures.
  • Early states: Kush and Aksum; the West African empires of Ghana, Mali (Mansa Musa, Timbuktu as a center of learning), and Songhai, enriched by trans-Saharan trade in gold and salt.
  • Other societies: the Swahili coast, Great Zimbabwe, and the Kongo kingdom.
  • Culture: griots preserved history orally; women held significant roles in many societies (for example, Queen Idia of Benin and Queen Njinga of Ndongo and Matamba).
  • Early contact with Europe: Portuguese trade along the West African coast in the 1400s led to the beginning of the Atlantic slave trade.
This unit counters the idea that African history begins with enslavement. Know the wealth, learning, and political complexity of African states.
