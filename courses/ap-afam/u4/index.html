Unit 4: Movements and Debates

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP African American Studies · Lesson 5 of 5 · 8 min read

  • The civil rights movement: Brown v. Board (1954), the Montgomery Bus Boycott (Rosa Parks, Martin Luther King Jr.), sit-ins (SNCC), the March on Washington (1963), and Selma. Results: the Civil Rights Act (1964) and Voting Rights Act (1965).
  • Black Power: Malcolm X, Stokely Carmichael, and the Black Panther Party emphasized self-defense, self-determination, and racial pride.
  • The Black Arts Movement and Black studies programs in universities.
  • Black feminism: the Combahee River Collective and writers like Audre Lorde examined how race, gender, and class intersect.
  • Political representation: Shirley Chisholm (first Black woman in Congress, 1968), the Congressional Black Caucus, and Barack Obama's election (2008).
  • Ongoing debates: reparations, mass incarceration, the Black Lives Matter movement, and the diversity of Black identities across the diaspora.
Tip On the exam, compare strategies: nonviolent direct action vs. Black Power, integration vs. self-determination. Explain the context that shaped each.
