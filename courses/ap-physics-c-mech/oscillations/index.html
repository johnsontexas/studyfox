Oscillations

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Physics C: Mechanics · Lesson 6 of 6 · 8 min read

  • SHM satisfies $\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = -\omega^2x$, with solution $x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$.
  • Spring: $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$. Simple pendulum: $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}$. Physical pendulum: $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{mgd}{I}}$.
  • Period $T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$.
  • Maximum speed is $A\omega$ and maximum acceleration is $A\omega^2$.
Tip To show something is SHM on an FRQ, write Newton's second law, get it into the form $\ddot{x} = -(\text{constant})x$, and read off $\omega^2$ as that constant.

Check your understanding

A mass on a spring has $\omega = 4$ rad/s and amplitude 0.5 m. What is its maximum speed?

  1. 0.125 m/s
  2. 2 m/s
  3. 8 m/s
  4. 4.5 m/s
Show answer

Answer: B. $v_{max} = A\omega = 0.5 \times 4 = 2$ m/s.
