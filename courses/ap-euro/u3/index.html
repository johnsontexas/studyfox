Unit 3: Absolutism and Constitutionalism

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP European History · Lesson 4 of 10 · 8 min read

Covers about 1648 to 1815.

Absolutism

  • Louis XIV of France ("I am the state") claimed divine right, built Versailles to control the nobles, and revoked the Edict of Nantes. His minister Colbert used mercantilism.
  • Peter the Great westernized Russia and built St. Petersburg.
  • Prussia built a powerful army with the support of the Junker nobility. Austria ruled a diverse Habsburg empire.

Constitutionalism

  • England: the conflict between the Stuart kings and Parliament led to the English Civil War, Charles I's execution, and Cromwell's rule. The Glorious Revolution (1688) and the English Bill of Rights (1689) established a constitutional monarchy with Parliament supreme.
  • The Dutch Republic: a wealthy, tolerant, commercial republic.

Balance of power

European states formed shifting alliances to stop any one power from dominating (the War of the Spanish Succession, the Seven Years' War).

Economy

The Agricultural Revolution (crop rotation, enclosure) and the putting-out (cottage) system set the stage for industrialization.
