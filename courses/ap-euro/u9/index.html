Unit 9: Cold War and Contemporary Europe

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP European History · Lesson 10 of 10 · 8 min read

  • A divided Europe: the "Iron Curtain" separated the democratic West and the Soviet-controlled East. NATO vs. the Warsaw Pact; the Berlin Wall (1961).
  • Recovery: the Marshall Plan rebuilt Western Europe. Western states built welfare states (national health care, pensions).
  • Integration: the European Coal and Steel Community (1951) grew into the European Economic Community and then the European Union (1993) and the euro.
  • Revolts in the East: Hungary (1956) and the Prague Spring (1968) were crushed. Poland's Solidarity union challenged communism in the 1980s.
  • The end of communism: Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika, the fall of the Berlin Wall (1989), and the collapse of the USSR (1991). Yugoslavia broke apart in violent ethnic wars.
  • Decolonization: Europe lost its empires (India 1947, Algeria 1962).
  • Recent issues: immigration, Brexit (2016), terrorism, climate policy, and Russia's wars in Ukraine.
