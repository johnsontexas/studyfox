Figurative language, symbols, and theme

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP English Literature and Composition · Lesson 5 of 6 · 6 min read

  • Metaphor and simile compare unlike things. Ask what qualities transfer from one to the other.
  • Symbols stand for something beyond themselves (the green light in The Great Gatsby).
  • Irony: verbal (saying the opposite of what you mean), situational (the opposite of what's expected), and dramatic (the audience knows what the character doesn't).
  • Theme is a statement about life, not a single word. "Love" is a topic; "Love can become possessive when it's built on fear" is a theme.
IncorrectThe theme of the story is loneliness.
CorrectThe story suggests that isolation can protect people from pain but also keeps them from the connections that would heal it.
