The exam and the essay rubric

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP English Literature and Composition · Lesson 1 of 6 · 6 min read

The exam: 55 multiple-choice questions on passages of prose and poetry (60 min, 45%), then 3 essays (2 hours, 55%): poetry analysis, prose fiction analysis, and a literary argument about a work you choose.

RowPointsWhat earns it
Thesis0 to 1A defensible claim that responds to the prompt
Evidence and commentary0 to 4Specific evidence, plus commentary that explains how it supports your line of reasoning
Sophistication0 to 1Complex understanding: tensions, broader context, or a consistently vivid, persuasive style
Tip For the literary argument (Q3), prepare 3 or 4 works you know deeply: plot, characters, key scenes, and themes. Novels and plays with rich conflicts work for almost any prompt.
