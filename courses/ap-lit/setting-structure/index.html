Setting, structure, and plot

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP English Literature and Composition · Lesson 3 of 6 · 6 min read

  • Setting can reflect a character's inner state, create conflict, or carry symbolic meaning (a storm, a closed room).
  • Structure: where the story starts and ends, flashbacks, foreshadowing, and pacing. Ask why the author arranged events this way.
  • Shifts in tone, time, or perspective often mark the most important moments of a passage.
  • Contrasts and juxtapositions create meaning by putting unlike things side by side.
Tip In a prose passage, look for the turn: the sentence where something changes. Analyze what comes before and after it.
