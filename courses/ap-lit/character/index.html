Character and narration

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP English Literature and Composition · Lesson 2 of 6 · 7 min read

  • Characterization: what a character says, does, thinks, and how others react. Notice when a character changes (dynamic) and what causes it.
  • Character foils highlight each other by contrast.
  • Narrator and point of view: first person (limited, possibly unreliable), third person limited, third person omniscient. Ask what the narrator knows, and what they don't see.
  • Unreliable narrators create a gap between what they say and what the reader understands; that gap is often the point.
Character questions usually come down to motive and conflict: what does this person want, and what stands in the way?
