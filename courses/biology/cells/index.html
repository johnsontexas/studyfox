Cells and organelles

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Biology Essentials · Lesson 1 of 5 · 6 min read

All living things are made of cells. Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) have no nucleus. Eukaryotic cells (plants, animals, fungi) have a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.

OrganelleJob
NucleusHolds DNA; controls the cell
RibosomeBuilds proteins
MitochondrionCellular respiration: makes ATP (usable energy)
Chloroplast (plants)Photosynthesis
Endoplasmic reticulumMakes and moves proteins (rough) and lipids (smooth)
Golgi apparatusPackages and ships proteins
Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves
Cell wall (plants)Rigid support
Tip Learn organelles by function, like a factory: the nucleus is the office, ribosomes are workers, the Golgi is shipping, and mitochondria are the power plant.

Which organelle produces most of a cell's ATP?

  1. Ribosome
  2. Golgi apparatus
  3. Mitochondrion
  4. Nucleus
Show answer

Answer: C. Mitochondria carry out cellular respiration, which produces most of the cell's ATP.
