Natural selection

  1. Individuals in a population vary.
  2. Some variation is inherited.
  3. More offspring are born than can survive.
  4. Individuals with helpful traits survive and reproduce more, so those traits become more common over generations.
Populations evolve, not individuals. An individual can't change its genes to adapt.

Energy in ecosystems

Energy flows from producers (plants) to primary consumers (herbivores) to secondary consumers. Only about 10% of the energy passes to each next level; the rest is used for life processes or lost as heat.
