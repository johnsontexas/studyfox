Photosynthesis and cellular respiration

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Biology Essentials · Lesson 2 of 5 · 6 min read

These two processes are mirror images of each other.

Photosynthesis (chloroplasts): 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2
Cellular respiration (mitochondria): C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + ATP
  • Plants do both. Animals only do respiration.
  • Photosynthesis stores energy in glucose; respiration releases it as ATP.
  • Without oxygen, cells use fermentation, which makes far less ATP.
