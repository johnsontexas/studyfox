Genetics and Punnett squares

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Biology Essentials · Lesson 4 of 5 · 7 min read

  • Alleles are versions of a gene. You inherit one from each parent.
  • Genotype is the allele pair (like Aa). Phenotype is the trait you see.
  • A dominant allele (A) shows up whenever it's present. A recessive allele (a) shows only in aa.
  • Homozygous: AA or aa. Heterozygous: Aa.
Example: Aa × Aa
Aa
AAAAa
aAaaa

Genotype ratio 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa. Phenotype ratio 3 dominant : 1 recessive.

Check your understanding

Two Aa parents have a child. What is the probability the child is aa?

  1. $0\%$
  2. $25\%$
  3. $50\%$
  4. $75\%$
Show answer

Answer: B. One of the four boxes in the Punnett square is aa, so the probability is $\frac{1}{4} = 25\%$.
