Unit 8: Cold War and Decolonization (1900 to present)

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP World History: Modern · Lesson 9 of 10 · 9 min read

The Cold War

  • The U.S. (capitalism, democracy) vs. the USSR (communism). Alliances: NATO vs. the Warsaw Pact.
  • Proxy wars in Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan, and Angola; the nuclear arms race; the Cuban Missile Crisis.
  • The Non-Aligned Movement (India's Nehru, Indonesia's Sukarno) refused to pick a side.
  • China: Mao's communist victory (1949), the Great Leap Forward (famine), and the Cultural Revolution.
  • The Cold War ended with the fall of the USSR in 1991, after Gorbachev's reforms (glasnost and perestroika).

Decolonization

  • Negotiated independence: India (1947, Gandhi's nonviolent resistance; partition with Pakistan caused mass violence and migration), Ghana (1957).
  • Armed struggle: Algeria against France, Vietnam against France, Kenya (the Mau Mau uprising), Angola against Portugal.
  • New states faced ethnic conflict, borders drawn by colonizers, and economic dependence. Some leaders pursued state-led development (Nasser in Egypt).
  • Apartheid in South Africa lasted until 1994, ended by resistance led by Nelson Mandela and the ANC and by international pressure.
