Slope and linear functions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Algebra 1 Essentials · Lesson 3 of 6 · 12 min read

Slope measures how steep a line is: how much $y$ changes for each 1-unit increase in $x$.

$m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$
  • Positive slope goes up to the right; negative slope goes down.
  • A horizontal line has slope 0. A vertical line has undefined slope.

Slope-intercept form

$y = mx + b$, where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the y-intercept (where the line crosses the y-axis).

In word problems, the slope is the rate (like dollars per hour) and the y-intercept is the starting value.

Check your understanding

What is the slope of the line through $(1, 3)$ and $(4, 9)$?

  1. $\frac{1}{2}$
  2. $2$
  3. $3$
  4. $6$
Show answer

Answer: B. $m = \frac{9 - 3}{4 - 1} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.
