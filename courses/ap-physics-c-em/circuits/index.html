Electric circuits and RC circuits

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism · Lesson 4 of 6 · 8 min read

  • Resistors, Ohm's law, and Kirchhoff's rules as in Physics 2. Resistivity $R = \frac{\rho L}{A}$.
  • Charging an RC circuit: $q(t) = C\varepsilon(1 - e^{-t/RC})$ and $I(t) = \frac{\varepsilon}{R}e^{-t/RC}$.
  • Discharging: $q(t) = Q_0e^{-t/RC}$.
  • The time constant $\tau = RC$: after one τ, a charging capacitor reaches about 63% of its final charge.
  • At $t = 0$ an uncharged capacitor acts like a wire; at steady state it acts like a break in the circuit.

Check your understanding

Immediately after a switch closes on an uncharged capacitor in series with a resistor, the capacitor acts like:

  1. An open circuit
  2. A short circuit (a wire)
  3. A battery
  4. An inductor
Show answer

Answer: B. With no charge, there's no voltage across the capacitor, so current flows as if it were a wire.
