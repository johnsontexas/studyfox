Unit 1: Biological Bases of Behavior

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Psychology · Lesson 2 of 6 · 9 min read

  • Neurons: dendrites receive, the axon sends, and neurotransmitters cross the synapse. An action potential is all-or-nothing.
  • Neurotransmitters: dopamine (reward, movement), serotonin (mood), acetylcholine (muscles, memory), GABA (inhibitory), glutamate (excitatory), endorphins (pain relief).
  • Nervous system: central (brain, spinal cord) and peripheral (somatic and autonomic: sympathetic is fight-or-flight, parasympathetic calms).
  • Brain regions: the frontal lobe (planning, judgment), parietal (touch), occipital (vision), temporal (hearing), cerebellum (coordination), amygdala (fear), hippocampus (new memories), hypothalamus (hunger, hormones). Broca's area (speech production) and Wernicke's area (language comprehension).
  • Sleep: NREM stages and REM (dreaming). Disorders: insomnia, narcolepsy, sleep apnea.
  • Sensation and perception: transduction, absolute and difference thresholds, sensory adaptation, and the parts of the eye and ear.
