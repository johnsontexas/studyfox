Unit 9: Thermodynamics and Electrochemistry

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Chemistry · Lesson 9 of 9 · 9 min read

  • Entropy (S) measures dispersal of matter and energy. It increases when solids become liquids or gases, and when gas moles increase.
  • Gibbs free energy: $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$. A reaction is thermodynamically favored if ΔG < 0.
  • If ΔH < 0 and ΔS > 0, it's favored at all temperatures. If both are positive, it's favored only at high T.
  • $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$: if K > 1, ΔG° < 0.
  • Galvanic cells: oxidation at the anode, reduction at the cathode ("An Ox, Red Cat"). Electrons flow from anode to cathode. E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode; positive means favored.
  • $\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ$. Electrolysis uses outside energy to drive unfavorable reactions: $q = It$.

A reaction has ΔH > 0 and ΔS > 0. When is it thermodynamically favored?

  1. At all temperatures
  2. At high temperatures only
  3. At low temperatures only
  4. Never

Answer: B. ΔG = ΔH − TΔS becomes negative only when TΔS is larger than ΔH, which happens at high T.
