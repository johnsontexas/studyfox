Unit 4: Chemical Reactions

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Chemistry · Lesson 4 of 9 · 8 min read

  • Physical vs. chemical changes: chemical changes make new substances (bonds break and form).
  • Net ionic equations: write only the species that react; remove spectator ions.
  • Stoichiometry: grams to moles (÷ molar mass), moles to moles (mole ratio from the balanced equation), moles to grams. The limiting reactant runs out first.
  • Titration: at the equivalence point, moles of acid equal moles of base (for a 1:1 reaction).
  • Reaction types: acid-base (proton transfer), redox (electron transfer; oxidation is loss, reduction is gain), and precipitation.
Limiting reactant

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. With 4 mol H₂ and 3 mol O₂: 4 mol H₂ needs only 2 mol O₂, so H₂ is limiting and forms 4 mol H₂O. 1 mol O₂ is left over.

Check your understanding

In the reaction Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu, which species is oxidized?

  1. Zn
  2. Cu²⁺
  3. Zn²⁺
  4. Cu
Show answer

Answer: A. Zinc loses two electrons, going from 0 to +2. Loss of electrons is oxidation.
