Unit 2: Compound Structure and Properties

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Chemistry · Lesson 2 of 9 · 8 min read

  • Bond types: ionic (metal and nonmetal, transfer), covalent (nonmetals, sharing; polar if electronegativities differ), and metallic (a sea of delocalized electrons).
  • Lattice energy is higher for ions with larger charges and smaller radii (Coulomb's law).
  • Lewis structures: use formal charge to choose the best structure. Resonance structures show delocalized electrons.
  • VSEPR: electron domains repel. Linear (2), trigonal planar (3), tetrahedral (4, 109.5°), trigonal bipyramidal (5), octahedral (6). Lone pairs compress bond angles (water is bent at about 104.5°).
  • Hybridization: sp (2 domains), sp² (3), sp³ (4). Sigma bonds are single bonds; a double bond is one sigma and one pi.

What is the molecular geometry of NH₃?

  1. Trigonal planar
  2. Trigonal pyramidal
  3. Tetrahedral
  4. Bent
Show answer

Answer: B. Nitrogen has four electron domains (three bonds and one lone pair), so the shape is trigonal pyramidal.
