Unit 5: Kinetics

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Chemistry · Lesson 5 of 9 · 8 min read

  • Rate law: rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ. Orders come from experiments, not from coefficients (unless it's an elementary step).
  • Find the order by comparing trials: if doubling [A] doubles the rate, it's first order; if it quadruples, second order; no change means zero order.
  • Integrated rate laws: zero order gives a linear [A] vs. t; first order gives a linear ln[A] vs. t; second order gives a linear 1/[A] vs. t.
  • Half-life of a first-order reaction is constant: $t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k}$.
  • Collision theory: particles must collide with enough energy and correct orientation.
  • Mechanisms: the slowest step determines the rate. Intermediates are made and then used; catalysts are used and then regenerated, and they lower activation energy.
