Unit 3: Cultural Patterns and Processes

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Human Geography · Lesson 3 of 7 · 8 min read

  • Culture: folk culture (local, traditional) vs. popular culture (widespread, changes fast). Globalization spreads popular culture and can threaten folk cultures.
  • Cultural landscape: the visible human imprint on the land, like buildings, signs, and religious structures.
  • Diffusion processes: relocation and expansion diffusion; acculturation (adopting traits while keeping your own), assimilation (fully adopting), syncretism (blending), and multiculturalism.
  • Religions: universalizing (seek converts: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Sikhism) vs. ethnic (tied to a group and place: Hinduism, Judaism). Know each one's hearth and how it spread.
  • Language: language families (Indo-European is the largest), lingua francas (English), and creole languages.
