Unit 6: Cities and Urban Land Use

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Human Geography · Lesson 6 of 7 · 8 min read

  • Urbanization: the share of people living in cities. Megacities (10 million or more) are growing fastest in developing countries.
  • City systems: the rank-size rule (the nth city is 1/n the size of the largest) vs. a primate city (more than twice the next largest, like Lima). Central place theory (Christaller) explains market areas.
  • Urban models: the concentric zone (Burgess), sector (Hoyt), multiple nuclei (Harris and Ullman), galactic city (edge cities), and models for Latin American, African, and Southeast Asian cities.
  • Issues: suburban sprawl, gentrification, redlining, squatter settlements, and infrastructure.
  • Responses: smart growth, New Urbanism (walkable, mixed-use neighborhoods), and urban growth boundaries.
