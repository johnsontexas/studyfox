Unit 7: Industrial and Economic Development

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Human Geography · Lesson 7 of 7 · 8 min read

  • Economic sectors: primary (extracting resources), secondary (manufacturing), tertiary (services), quaternary (information), and quinary (top-level decision-making).
  • Measures of development: GDP and GNI per capita, the Human Development Index (income, education, life expectancy), the Gender Inequality Index, and the Gini coefficient.
  • Theories: Rostow's stages (traditional society to high mass consumption), Wallerstein's world-systems theory (core, semi-periphery, periphery), and dependency theory.
  • Weber's least-cost theory: industries locate to minimize transport, labor, and agglomeration costs. Bulk-reducing industries locate near raw materials; bulk-gaining near markets.
  • Globalization: outsourcing, special economic zones, export processing zones, and maquiladoras. Deindustrialization in core countries.
  • Sustainable development: the UN Sustainable Development Goals; microloans that help people, especially women, start businesses.
