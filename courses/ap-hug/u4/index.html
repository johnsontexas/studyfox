Unit 4: Political Patterns and Processes

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Human Geography · Lesson 4 of 7 · 8 min read

  • Key terms: state (sovereign country), nation (people with shared culture), nation-state (Japan, Iceland), multinational state (Russia), stateless nation (the Kurds, Palestinians).
  • Boundaries: geometric (straight lines), physical (rivers, mountains), and cultural. Antecedent, subsequent, superimposed (imposed by outsiders, like many African borders), and relic boundaries.
  • State shapes: compact, elongated (Chile), fragmented (Indonesia), perforated (South Africa around Lesotho), prorupted.
  • Forces: centripetal (unify: shared language, national symbols) vs. centrifugal (divide: ethnic conflict, uneven development).
  • Devolution: power moving to regions (Scotland, Catalonia). Supranationalism: states joining organizations like the EU and the UN.
  • Electoral geography: redistricting and gerrymandering (packing and cracking).
  • Law of the Sea: territorial waters to 12 nautical miles; exclusive economic zone to 200.
