Building a research question and method

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Seminar and AP Research · Lesson 3 of 4 · 7 min read

  • A good research question is focused, researchable, and fills a gap in what is known. "How does social media affect teens?" is too broad. "How does nightly phone use after 10 p.m. relate to sleep quality among students at my school?" is focused.
  • Literature review: summarize what researchers already know and identify the gap your project addresses.
  • Choose a method that fits the question: surveys, interviews, experiments, content analysis, or case studies.
  • Ethics: research with people needs informed consent and approval from your school's review board.
  • Limitations: every study has them (small sample, self-reported data). Naming them shows you understand your results.
