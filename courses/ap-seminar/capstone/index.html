AP Seminar and AP Research · Lesson 1 of 4 · 5 min read

AP Capstone is two courses taken in order: AP Seminar, then AP Research. Students who score 3 or higher on both, plus four other AP exams, earn the AP Capstone Diploma.

  • AP Seminar: a team project and presentation, an individual research essay and presentation, and an end-of-course written exam.
  • AP Research: a year-long independent research project, resulting in an academic paper of about 4,000 to 5,000 words and a presentation with an oral defense. There is no end-of-course exam.

Both courses use the QUEST framework: Question and explore, Understand and analyze, Evaluate multiple perspectives, Synthesize ideas, Team, transform, and transmit.
