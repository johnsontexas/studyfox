Finding and evaluating sources

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Seminar and AP Research · Lesson 2 of 4 · 7 min read

  • Use scholarly sources: peer-reviewed journals, academic books, and government data. Library databases and Google Scholar help.
  • Evaluate each source: the author's credentials, the publisher, the date, the evidence used, and possible bias or funding conflicts.
  • Lateral reading: check what other trusted sources say about a website or author, instead of trusting the site's own "About" page.
  • Separate claims from evidence. Note what the source actually shows versus what it argues.
Tip Keep an annotated bibliography from day one: the citation, a two-sentence summary, and a note on how credible it is and how you might use it.
