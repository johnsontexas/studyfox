Circles

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Geometry Essentials · Lesson 5 of 5 · 6 min read

  • Arc length $= \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot 2\pi r$ and sector area $= \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot \pi r^2$, where $\theta$ is the central angle in degrees.
  • An inscribed angle (vertex on the circle) is half the arc it intercepts. A central angle equals its arc.
  • A tangent line is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency.

Circles on the coordinate plane

$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$ has center $(h, k)$ and radius $r$.

If the equation is expanded, complete the square in $x$ and in $y$ to get it back into this form.

Check your understanding

An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of $100^\circ$. What is the measure of the angle?

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $100^\circ$
  3. $200^\circ$
  4. $80^\circ$
Show answer

Answer: A. An inscribed angle is half its intercepted arc: $100 \div 2 = 50$.
