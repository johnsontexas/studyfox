Area and volume

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Geometry Essentials · Lesson 4 of 5 · 6 min read

ShapeFormula
Rectangle$A = lw$
Triangle$A = \frac{1}{2}bh$
Trapezoid$A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$
Circle$A = \pi r^2$, $C = 2\pi r$
Prism or cylinder$V = Bh$ (base area times height), so a cylinder is $\pi r^2 h$
Pyramid or cone$V = \frac{1}{3}Bh$, so a cone is $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
Sphere$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, $SA = 4\pi r^2$
Tip Height is always measured perpendicular to the base, not along a slanted side.

Check your understanding

What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 3 and height 5?

  1. $15\pi$
  2. $30\pi$
  3. $45\pi$
  4. $75\pi$
Show answer

Answer: C. $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi (9)(5) = 45\pi$.
