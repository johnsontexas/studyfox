Congruence and similarity

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Geometry Essentials · Lesson 3 of 5 · 5 min read

Congruent figures are identical in size and shape. Similar figures have the same shape but may be different sizes.

  • Triangles are congruent by SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or HL (right triangles). SSA and AAA don't prove congruence.
  • Triangles are similar if two pairs of angles match (AA). Corresponding sides are then proportional.
If the scale factor between similar figures is $k$: lengths multiply by $k$, areas by $k^2$, and volumes by $k^3$.

Check your understanding

Two similar triangles have a scale factor of 3. The smaller has an area of 5. What is the area of the larger?

  1. $15$
  2. $45$
  3. $135$
  4. $8$
Show answer

Answer: B. Areas scale by $k^2 = 9$: $5 \times 9 = 45$.
