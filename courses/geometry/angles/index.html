Angles and parallel lines

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Geometry Essentials · Lesson 1 of 5 · 5 min read

  • Complementary angles add to $90^\circ$. Supplementary angles add to $180^\circ$.
  • Vertical angles (across from each other where two lines cross) are equal.
  • Angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$. Angles around a point add to $360^\circ$.

Parallel lines cut by a transversal

Corresponding angles and alternate interior angles are equal. Same-side interior angles are supplementary.

In practice: every angle in the diagram is one of just two sizes, and those two sizes add to $180^\circ$.

Check your understanding

Two angles are supplementary. One measures $65^\circ$. What does the other measure?

  1. $25^\circ$
  2. $115^\circ$
  3. $65^\circ$
  4. $295^\circ$
Show answer

Answer: B. Supplementary angles add to $180^\circ$: $180 - 65 = 115$.
