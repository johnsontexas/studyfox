Period 8: 1945 to 1980

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP U.S. History · Lesson 9 of 10 · 10 min read

The Cold War

  • Containment: the Truman Doctrine (1947), Marshall Plan, NATO (1949), the Berlin Airlift.
  • Hot wars: Korea (1950 to 1953) and Vietnam (Gulf of Tonkin Resolution 1964, Tet Offensive 1968, withdrawal 1973).
  • The arms race, Sputnik, and the Cuban Missile Crisis (1962). Nixon's détente with the USSR and opening to China.
  • At home: the second Red Scare and McCarthyism.

Civil rights

  • Brown v. Board of Education (1954) overturned "separate but equal" in schools.
  • The Montgomery Bus Boycott (1955), sit-ins, Freedom Rides, the March on Washington (1963), and Selma (1965).
  • Civil Rights Act (1964) banned discrimination in public places and employment; Voting Rights Act (1965) banned literacy tests and added federal oversight.
  • Other movements: Black Power, the women's movement (NOW, the Equal Rights Amendment fight), the Chicano movement (César Chávez), the American Indian Movement, and gay rights after Stonewall (1969).

Society and politics

Postwar prosperity, the GI Bill, suburbs, and the baby boom. LBJ's Great Society created Medicare, Medicaid, and the War on Poverty. The counterculture and antiwar protests divided the country. Watergate led to Nixon's resignation (1974), and the 1970s brought inflation and the energy crisis.

  3. Rolling back communism through direct invasion of the USSR
  4. Limiting immigration from Europe
