Period 7: 1890 to 1945

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP U.S. History · Lesson 8 of 10 · 10 min read

Imperialism

The Spanish-American War (1898) gave the U.S. Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Philippines, where a long war against Filipino independence fighters followed. The Open Door policy (China), the Roosevelt Corollary, and the Panama Canal expanded U.S. power.

The Progressive Era

  • Muckrakers exposed problems: Upton Sinclair's The Jungle led to the Meat Inspection Act and Pure Food and Drug Act (1906).
  • Reforms: trust-busting, the 16th (income tax), 17th (direct election of senators), 18th (Prohibition), and 19th (women's suffrage, 1920) Amendments.
  • Presidents: Theodore Roosevelt (Square Deal), Taft, Wilson (New Freedom, Federal Reserve).

World War I and the 1920s

The U.S. entered WWI in 1917. At home: the Espionage and Sedition Acts, the Great Migration of Black Americans north, and the first Red Scare. The Senate rejected the Treaty of Versailles and the League of Nations. The 1920s brought consumer culture, the Harlem Renaissance, immigration quotas (1924), and cultural clashes like the Scopes Trial.

The Great Depression and New Deal

The 1929 crash and bank failures led to 25% unemployment. FDR's New Deal pursued "relief, recovery, and reform": the CCC, WPA, FDIC, SEC, Social Security, and the Wagner Act protecting unions. It greatly expanded the federal government's role.

World War II

After Pearl Harbor (1941) the U.S. fully mobilized: women entered factories, the Great Migration continued, and the economy recovered. Japanese American internment (upheld in Korematsu v. U.S.) is a key civil liberties issue. The war ended with the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945.

Check your understanding

Which best describes the overall effect of the New Deal?

  1. It ended the Great Depression by 1934
  2. It permanently expanded the federal government's role in the economy and social welfare
  3. It returned the U.S. to laissez-faire policies
  4. It was mostly struck down and had no lasting effect
Show answer

Answer: B. Programs like Social Security, the FDIC, and the SEC permanently expanded what Americans expected the federal government to do. Full recovery came with WWII.
