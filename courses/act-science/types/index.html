The three passage types

ACT Science · Lesson 1 of 4 · 6 min read

On the enhanced ACT, Science is an optional section with 40 questions in 40 minutes. You don't need to memorize much science; nearly every answer is in the passage, table, or graph.

TypeWhat it looks likeHow to approach it
Data representationGraphs and tables with a short introGo straight to the questions and look up values
Research summariesSeveral experiments with resultsNote what changes between experiments
Conflicting viewpointsTwo or more scientists or students disagreeRead and summarize each viewpoint
Tip For data passages, skim the intro in a few seconds and go to the questions. Most can be answered from the figures alone.
