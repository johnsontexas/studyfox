Experiments: variables and controls

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT Science · Lesson 3 of 4 · 8 min read

TermMeaningExample
Independent variableWhat the scientist changes on purposeAmount of fertilizer
Dependent variableWhat is measuredPlant height
Controlled variablesKept the same in every trialLight, water, soil type
Control groupGets no treatment, for comparisonPlants with no fertilizer

Common questions

  • "Why did the scientist do Experiment 2?" Find what is different between Experiments 1 and 2. That variable is what Experiment 2 was testing.
  • "Which change would let the scientist test X?" The right design changes only X and keeps everything else the same.
  • "What would happen if..." Use the trend from the results to predict.
A fair test changes one variable at a time. If two things change at once, you can't tell which one caused the result.

Check your understanding

A student tests how water temperature affects how fast sugar dissolves. What is the dependent variable?

  1. Water temperature
  2. Time for the sugar to dissolve
  3. Type of sugar
  4. Size of the cup
Show answer

Answer: B. The dependent variable is what is measured: how long the sugar takes to dissolve.
