Reading graphs and tables

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT Science · Lesson 2 of 4 · 8 min read

  1. Read the labels and units on every axis and column before looking at values.
  2. Find the trend. As one variable increases, does the other increase, decrease, or stay the same?
  3. Interpolate and extrapolate. Questions may ask for a value between two data points (estimate between them) or beyond the data (continue the trend).
Example

A table shows a plant's height at 10, 20, and 30 days as 4 cm, 8 cm, and 12 cm. What was its height at 25 days?

The height rises 4 cm every 10 days, so at 25 days it is about 10 cm.

When a question names a specific figure ("According to Figure 2"), use only that figure.
Tip Watch for two y-axes on one graph. Check which line goes with which axis.

Check your understanding

In a table, temperature readings are 20°C at 1 minute, 30°C at 2 minutes, and 40°C at 3 minutes. If the trend continues, what is the temperature at 4 minutes?

  1. 40°C
  2. 45°C
  3. 50°C
  4. 60°C
Show answer

Answer: C. The temperature rises 10°C each minute, so at 4 minutes it should be about 50°C.
