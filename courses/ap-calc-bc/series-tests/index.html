Infinite series and convergence tests (BC)

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Calculus: Exam Guide and BC Topics · Lesson 4 of 5 · 10 min read

  • nth term test: if $a_n \not\to 0$, the series diverges. (If $a_n \to 0$, the test tells you nothing.)
  • Geometric series: $\sum ar^n$ converges to $\frac{a}{1 - r}$ if $|r| < 1$.
  • p-series: $\sum\frac{1}{n^p}$ converges if $p > 1$. The harmonic series ($p = 1$) diverges.
  • Integral, comparison, and limit comparison tests.
  • Alternating series test: converges if terms decrease in size to 0. Error bound: less than the first omitted term.
  • Ratio test: $\lim\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| < 1$ converges; $> 1$ diverges; $= 1$ is inconclusive.
  • Absolute vs. conditional convergence: $\sum\frac{(-1)^n}{n}$ converges conditionally.

Check your understanding

What is the sum of $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 3\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n$?

  1. $3$
  2. $6$
  3. $\frac{3}{2}$
  4. It diverges
Show answer

Answer: B. Geometric with $a = 3$ and $r = \frac{1}{2}$: sum $= \frac{3}{1 - 1/2} = 6$.
