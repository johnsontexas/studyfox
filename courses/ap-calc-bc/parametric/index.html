Parametric, vector, and polar calculus (BC)

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Calculus: Exam Guide and BC Topics · Lesson 3 of 5 · 9 min read

  • Parametric slope: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$. Second derivative: $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{\frac{d}{dt}(dy/dx)}{dx/dt}$.
  • Arc length: $\int_a^b \sqrt{(x'(t))^2 + (y'(t))^2}\,dt$.
  • Motion with vectors: velocity $\langle x'(t), y'(t)\rangle$; speed is its magnitude; total distance is the integral of speed.
  • Polar area: $A = \frac{1}{2}\int_\alpha^\beta r^2\,d\theta$.
  • Polar slope: use $x = r\cos\theta$, $y = r\sin\theta$ and the parametric formula.

Check your understanding

If $x = t^2$ and $y = t^3$, what is $\frac{dy}{dx}$?

  1. $\frac{3t}{2}$
  2. $\frac{2}{3t}$
  3. $6t^2$
  4. $t$
Show answer

Answer: A. $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3t^2}{2t} = \frac{3t}{2}$.
