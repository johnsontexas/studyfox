Multiple choice and free response are each 50%. BC covers all of AB plus about 40% more; BC students also get an AB subscore.

  • Show your setup: write the integral or derivative before using a calculator. A bare number gets little credit.
  • Units and meaning: many FRQs ask what a value means in context, with units.
  • Justify using theorems by name: Mean Value Theorem, Intermediate Value Theorem, Extreme Value Theorem, and the first and second derivative tests.
