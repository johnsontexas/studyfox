Big Idea 5: Impact of Computing

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Computer Science Principles · Lesson 6 of 6 · 7 min read

  • Beneficial and harmful effects: innovations often have unintended consequences.
  • The digital divide: unequal access to computing based on income, geography, and other factors.
  • Bias in computing: algorithms trained on biased data can produce biased results.
  • Crowdsourcing and citizen science gather contributions from many people.
  • Legal and ethical: intellectual property, Creative Commons licenses, and open source software.
  • Security: personally identifiable information (PII), phishing, malware, and keylogging. Encryption: symmetric (one shared key) vs. public key (a public key encrypts, a private key decrypts). Multifactor authentication requires more than one kind of proof.

In public key encryption, which key decrypts a message?

  1. The sender's public key
  2. The recipient's private key
  3. A shared symmetric key
  4. No key is needed
Show answer

Answer: B. Anyone can encrypt with the recipient's public key, but only the recipient's private key decrypts it.
