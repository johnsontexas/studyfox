Big Idea 4: Computer Systems and Networks

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Computer Science Principles · Lesson 5 of 6 · 7 min read

  • The internet is a network of networks using open protocols: IP (addressing and routing), TCP (reliable delivery, reassembles packets in order), UDP (faster, no guarantee), and HTTP (the web).
  • Data is split into packets that may take different routes and arrive out of order.
  • Fault tolerance: redundant paths let the internet keep working when parts fail.
  • Bandwidth is the maximum data per second.
  • Parallel and distributed computing speed up tasks. Speedup = sequential time ÷ parallel time, limited by the longest sequential part.
  • The internet vs. the World Wide Web: the web is one service (web pages) that runs on the internet.
