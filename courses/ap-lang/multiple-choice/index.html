Multiple choice: reading and composition

AP English Language and Composition · Lesson 6 of 6 · 6 min read

The exam has two kinds of passages:

  • Reading questions ask about the writer's purpose, the function of a sentence or paragraph, tone, and how the argument is built.
  • Composition questions treat the passage as a draft and ask how to revise it: which sentence best supports the claim, where to add a transition, which evidence fits the audience.
Tip For composition questions, reread the goal in the question stem ("to strengthen the claim about cost..."). The right answer does exactly that, even if other choices are well written.

Check your understanding

A composition question asks which revision best "establishes the writer's credibility." What should you look for?

  1. The most emotional sentence
  2. A sentence showing the writer's experience or expertise
  3. The shortest sentence
  4. A statistic about a different topic
Show answer

Answer: B. Credibility (ethos) comes from the writer's relevant experience, knowledge, or fairness.
