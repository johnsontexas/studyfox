Transitions

Time orderLater, Subsequently, Afterward
SimilaritySimilarly, Likewise
Tip Two choices with the same meaning (like "Therefore" and "Thus") can't both be right, so both are usually wrong.

Rhetorical synthesis (student notes)

You get bullet-point notes and a goal, like "emphasize a difference" or "introduce the topic to a new audience." Read the goal first and ignore the notes until you know what you need.

  1. Underline the key words in the goal: contrast, emphasize, explain why, introduce.
  2. Eliminate every choice that doesn't do that job, even if it is accurate.
  3. For "audience unfamiliar with" goals, the answer must explain who or what the subject is.
In synthesis questions every choice is usually true. The only question is which one meets the goal.

Check your understanding

"The bakery ran out of flour early. ______, it closed at noon." Which transition fits?

  1. However
  2. As a result
  3. For example
  4. Similarly
Show answer

Answer: B. Closing early was caused by running out of flour, so the relationship is cause and effect: "As a result."
