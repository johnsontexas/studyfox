How the section works

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Reading and Writing · Lesson 1 of 6 · 6 min read

The Reading and Writing section has two modules of 27 questions each, with 32 minutes per module. That is about 70 seconds per question. Every question has its own short passage, usually 25 to 150 words, so you never have to hold a long article in your head.

DomainShare of sectionQuestion types
Craft and Structureabout 28%Words in context, text structure and purpose, cross-text connections
Information and Ideasabout 26%Central ideas, inferences, command of evidence (text and data)
Standard English Conventionsabout 26%Boundaries (punctuation), form, structure, and sense (grammar)
Expression of Ideasabout 20%Transitions, rhetorical synthesis (student notes)

The order is predictable

Questions appear grouped by type in roughly the order above: vocabulary first, grammar near the end. Within each group, questions get harder. If a group is going badly, you can flag it and move on, then come back.

The correct answer is always supported by the text. If you cannot point to the words that prove a choice, it is not the answer.
Tip Grammar and transition questions are the fastest. If time is short at the end of a module, make sure you have answered those.
