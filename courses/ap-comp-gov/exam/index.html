The exam: 55 multiple-choice questions (60 min, 50%) and 4 free-response questions (90 min, 50%): conceptual analysis, quantitative analysis, comparative analysis, and an argument essay.

Tip For every concept, have a ready example from two different countries. Comparison is the core skill.

Which of the six countries has a parliamentary system?

  1. Mexico
  2. Nigeria
  3. United Kingdom
