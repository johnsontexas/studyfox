Unit 3: Political Culture and Participation

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Comparative Government · Lesson 4 of 6 · 7 min read

  • Civil society: voluntary groups outside government. Strong in the UK and Mexico; tightly controlled in China, Russia, and Iran.
  • Political culture and ideology: liberalism, socialism, communism, fascism, and religious ideologies (Iran's Shia theocracy).
  • Political socialization: authoritarian states use state media and education to shape beliefs (China's "Xi Jinping Thought").
  • Cleavages: divisions like ethnicity, religion, region, and class. Nigeria has strong ethnic (Hausa-Fulani, Yoruba, Igbo) and religious (Muslim North, Christian South) cleavages. The UK has national cleavages (Scotland).
  • Civil rights and liberties: restricted in authoritarian states through censorship, internet controls, and limits on protest (Iran's 2022 protests, Russia's laws on "foreign agents").
