Unit 1: Political Systems, Regimes, and Governments

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Comparative Government · Lesson 2 of 6 · 7 min read

  • State, nation, regime, government: a state is a territory with a sovereign government; a nation is a people with shared identity; a regime is the set of rules for how power works; a government is the people in power right now.
  • Legitimacy is the belief that the government has the right to rule. Sources: traditional, charismatic, and rational-legal (Max Weber), plus economic performance (China) and religion (Iran).
  • Sovereignty can be weakened by supranational organizations (the EU) or by armed groups (Boko Haram in Nigeria).
  • Democratization: free and fair elections, rule of law, and civil liberties. Mexico's 2000 election ended PRI dominance. Democratic backsliding: Russia under Putin.
  • Federal vs. unitary: Mexico, Nigeria, and Russia are federal; the UK (with devolution to Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland), China, and Iran are unitary.
