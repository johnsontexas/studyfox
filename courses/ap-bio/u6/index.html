Unit 6: Gene Expression and Regulation

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Biology · Lesson 6 of 8 · 9 min read

  • DNA replication is semiconservative. Helicase unwinds, DNA polymerase adds nucleotides 5' to 3', the leading strand is continuous, the lagging strand forms Okazaki fragments, and ligase joins them.
  • Transcription (nucleus): RNA polymerase makes mRNA. In eukaryotes, mRNA gets a 5' cap, a poly-A tail, and introns are spliced out. Alternative splicing lets one gene make several proteins.
  • Translation (ribosome): codons are read in sets of three; tRNA anticodons bring amino acids.
  • Gene regulation: prokaryotic operons (the lac operon is inducible; the trp operon is repressible). Eukaryotes use transcription factors, enhancers, and epigenetic changes (DNA methylation, histone acetylation).
  • Mutations: substitutions (silent, missense, nonsense) and frameshifts (insertions or deletions, usually more harmful).
  • Biotechnology: PCR (copies DNA), gel electrophoresis (smaller fragments move farther), and bacterial transformation.
