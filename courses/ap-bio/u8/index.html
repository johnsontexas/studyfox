Unit 8: Ecology

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Biology · Lesson 8 of 8 · 8 min read

  • Behavior: organisms respond to their environment (taxis, kinesis) and communicate.
  • Energy flow: only about 10% of energy passes to the next trophic level. Gross vs. net primary productivity.
  • Population growth: exponential, $\frac{dN}{dt} = r_{max}N$, vs. logistic, $\frac{dN}{dt} = r_{max}N\frac{K - N}{K}$, which levels off at carrying capacity K.
  • Communities: species interactions (competition, predation, mutualism, commensalism, parasitism), keystone species, and the Simpson diversity index.
  • Biodiversity makes ecosystems more resilient. Disruptions include invasive species, habitat loss, and climate change.
  • Cycles: carbon, nitrogen (nitrogen fixation by bacteria), phosphorus (no atmospheric phase), and water.

Check your understanding

In logistic growth, what happens to the growth rate as N approaches K?

  1. It increases
  2. It approaches zero
  3. It becomes negative immediately
  4. It stays constant
Show answer

Answer: B. The factor (K − N)/K approaches 0 as N nears K, so growth slows and levels off.
