The exam format and the six themes

AP World Languages: Exam Skills · Lesson 1 of 4 · 6 min read

AP Spanish, French, German, and Italian Language and Culture share a format:

SectionTasksWeight
Multiple choiceReading print texts, then audio and combined print-and-audio sources50%
Free response: writingEmail reply (15 min) and argumentative essay using 3 sources (about 55 min)25%
Free response: speakingSimulated conversation (5 turns, 20 seconds each) and a 2-minute cultural comparison25%

All the courses are built around six themes: families and communities, personal and public identities, beauty and aesthetics, science and technology, contemporary life, and global challenges.

Tip AP Chinese and Japanese use a different, computer-based format (including story narration and text chat), and AP Latin focuses on translating Vergil and Caesar. AP Spanish Literature reads a required list of works. Check the College Board page for your exact exam.
