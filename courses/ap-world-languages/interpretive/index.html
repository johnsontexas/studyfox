Reading and listening

AP World Languages: Exam Skills · Lesson 2 of 4 · 6 min read

  • Preview the questions before reading or listening, so you know what to look for.
  • Use the introduction: each source has a short intro telling you its topic and origin.
  • Listen for structure: the main idea, examples, and the speaker's attitude. Take quick notes during the first listen.
  • Use cognates and context for unfamiliar words; don't panic about one word.
Build the skill daily: podcasts, news, and videos in the language, even 10 minutes a day, add up fast.
