The email reply and argumentative essay

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP World Languages: Exam Skills · Lesson 3 of 4 · 7 min read

Email reply

  • Use the formal register (usted, vous, Sie, Lei), a greeting, and a closing.
  • Answer every question asked, and ask for more detail about something mentioned in the email.

Argumentative essay

  • Read the two print sources and listen to the audio source (played twice).
  • Take a clear position, cite all three sources, and organize with transitions.
  • Use a range of tenses and structures (including the subjunctive where it applies) to show control of the language.

Check your understanding

In the email reply, what must you include besides answering the questions?

  1. A poem
  2. A request for more details about something in the email
  3. A list of vocabulary
  4. An informal greeting
Show answer

Answer: B. The task asks you to request more information, and you should use a formal register throughout.
