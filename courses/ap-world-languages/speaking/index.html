Conversation and cultural comparison

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP World Languages: Exam Skills · Lesson 4 of 4 · 7 min read

Simulated conversation

  • Read the outline of the conversation before it starts; it tells you what each turn requires.
  • Speak for the full 20 seconds. Elaborate with a reason or example.

Cultural comparison (2 minutes)

  • You get 4 minutes to prepare and 2 minutes to speak on a question about a cultural topic.
  • Compare your own community with a community where the language is spoken. Give specific cultural examples, not stereotypes.
  • Structure: introduction, the target culture, your community, similarities and differences, conclusion.
Tip Prepare a set of cultural examples in advance for each of the six themes, such as festivals, education systems, environmental programs, and family traditions.

Check your understanding

What makes a strong cultural comparison?

  1. General statements about everyone in a country
  2. Specific examples from both your community and the target culture
  3. Only describing your own community
  4. Reading a prepared script
Show answer

Answer: B. Specific, accurate examples from both cultures, with a clear comparison, earn the highest scores.
