Atoms and the periodic table

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Chemistry Essentials · Lesson 1 of 5 · 6 min read

  • Protons (+) and neutrons (no charge) are in the nucleus. Electrons (−) surround it.
  • Atomic number = number of protons. It defines the element.
  • Mass number = protons + neutrons. Isotopes have the same protons but different neutrons.
  • A neutral atom has equal protons and electrons. Ions have gained or lost electrons.

Reading the periodic table

Columns are groups: elements in a group have the same number of valence electrons and similar behavior. Rows are periods.

Trends: atomic radius gets bigger down a group and smaller across a period. Electronegativity is the opposite: highest at the top right (fluorine).

Check your understanding

Carbon-14 has atomic number 6. How many neutrons does it have?

  1. $6$
  2. $8$
  3. $14$
  4. $20$
Show answer

Answer: B. Neutrons = mass number − protons = 14 − 6 = 8.
