Acids, bases, and pH

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

Chemistry Essentials · Lesson 5 of 5 · 5 min read

  • Acids release H+ ions in water. Bases release OH− or accept H+.
  • pH below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral, above 7 is basic.
  • $\text{pH} = -\log[\text{H}^+]$
pH is a log scale: each step of 1 is a 10 times change in H+ concentration.

Acid + base → salt + water. This is called neutralization.

How much more acidic is a solution with pH 3 than one with pH 5?

  1. 2 times
  2. 20 times
  3. 100 times
  4. 1,000 times
Show answer

Answer: C. Two pH steps, each a factor of 10: $10 \times 10 = 100$.
