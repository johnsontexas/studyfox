Exponential growth and decay

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

SAT Math: Algebra and Advanced Math · Lesson 5 of 6 · 7 min read

An exponential function multiplies by the same factor over equal time steps: $f(t) = a \cdot b^t$.

$a$ is the starting amount. If $b > 1$ the quantity grows; if $0 < b < 1$ it decays. Growth by $r$ percent means $b = 1 + r$; decay by $r$ percent means $b = 1 - r$.
Example

A town of 8,000 people grows by 3% per year. Write a model.

$P(t) = 8000(1.03)^t$. After 10 years, $P \approx 10{,}751$.

Linear or exponential?

Linear changes by the same amount each step (add 50 each year). Exponential changes by the same percent or factor each step (double each year, lose 10% each year).

Tip Watch the time units. If something doubles every 3 years, the model is $a \cdot 2^{t/3}$ when $t$ is in years.

Check your understanding

A car worth \$20,000 loses 15% of its value each year. Which expression gives its value after $t$ years?

  1. $20000(0.15)^t$
  2. $20000(0.85)^t$
  3. $20000(1.15)^t$
  4. $20000 - 0.15t$
Show answer

Answer: B. Losing 15% leaves 85% each year, so multiply by $0.85$ each year.
