Linear inequalities

SAT Math: Algebra and Advanced Math · Lesson 3 of 6 · 6 min read

Solve an inequality like an equation, with one extra rule:

When you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.

Word problems

Translate carefully. "At least" means $\ge$, "at most" and "no more than" mean $\le$, and "more than" means $>$.

Example

A van can carry at most 1,200 pounds. The driver weighs 180 pounds, and each box weighs 60 pounds. What is the most boxes the van can carry?

$180 + 60b \le 1200$, so $60b \le 1020$ and $b \le 17$. The answer is 17 boxes.

Graphs of inequalities

A solid line means the boundary is included ($\le$ or $\ge$); a dashed line means it is not. To check a point, plug it into the inequality, or type it into Desmos and see whether it falls in the shaded region.

Check your understanding

Solve $-3x + 4 > 10$.

  1. $x > -2$
  2. $x < -2$
  3. $x > 2$
  4. $x < 2$
Show answer

Answer: B. $-3x > 6$. Dividing by $-3$ flips the sign: $x < -2$.
