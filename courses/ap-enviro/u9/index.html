Unit 9: Global Change

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Environmental Science · Lesson 9 of 9 · 7 min read

  • Stratospheric ozone depletion: CFCs released chlorine that destroys ozone. The Montreal Protocol (1987) phased them out, and the ozone layer is recovering.
  • The greenhouse effect: CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, and water vapor trap heat. Methane and nitrous oxide have much higher warming potential per molecule than CO₂.
  • Effects of climate change: rising sea levels, melting ice, more extreme weather, shifting habitats, and coral bleaching.
  • Ocean acidification: oceans absorb CO₂, forming carbonic acid, which makes it harder for corals and shellfish to build shells.
  • Invasive species (kudzu, zebra mussels) outcompete natives. Endangered species are protected by laws like the Endangered Species Act and CITES.
  • Solutions: renewable energy, carbon taxes, protected areas, and habitat corridors.
