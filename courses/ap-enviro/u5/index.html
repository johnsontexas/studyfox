Unit 5: Land and Water Use

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Environmental Science · Lesson 5 of 9 · 8 min read

  • Tragedy of the commons: shared resources get overused when individuals act in self-interest (overfishing, overgrazing).
  • Forestry: clear-cutting vs. selective cutting. Deforestation reduces carbon storage and habitat.
  • Agriculture: the Green Revolution, monocultures, tillage (which increases erosion), irrigation (risk of salinization and waterlogging), and pest control (integrated pest management reduces pesticides).
  • Meat production: CAFOs (concentrated animal feeding operations) are efficient but produce concentrated waste.
  • Mining: surface mining disturbs more land; reclamation restores it.
  • Urbanization: impervious surfaces increase runoff and flooding. Urban sprawl increases driving.
  • Sustainable practices: crop rotation, contour plowing, terracing, no-till farming, aquaculture, and sustainable forestry.
