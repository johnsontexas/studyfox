Unit 3: Populations

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

AP Environmental Science · Lesson 3 of 9 · 7 min read

  • r-selected species (insects, mice): many offspring, little care, fast growth. K-selected species (elephants, humans): few offspring, much care, populations near carrying capacity.
  • Survivorship curves: Type I (most survive to old age), Type II (constant death rate), Type III (most die young).
  • Carrying capacity (K) is limited by density-dependent factors (food, disease) and density-independent ones (natural disasters).
  • Human population: age structure diagrams, total fertility rate, and the demographic transition.
  • Rule of 70: doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate (in percent).
Doubling time

A population grows 2% per year. Doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ 2 = 35 years.

Check your understanding

A population grows at 3.5% per year. About how long until it doubles?

  1. 10 years
  2. 20 years
  3. 35 years
  4. 70 years

Answer: B. Rule of 70: 70 ÷ 3.5 = 20 years.
