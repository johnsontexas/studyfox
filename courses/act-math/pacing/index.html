Pacing and formulas to memorize

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT Math · Lesson 1 of 6 · 6 min read

The enhanced ACT Math test has 45 questions in 50 minutes, a little over a minute each. Questions get harder as you go, so the early ones should be quick. You can use an approved calculator on every question.

The ACT does not give a formula sheet. You must memorize the formulas below.
TopicFormula
Slope$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
Midpoint$\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$
Distance$\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
Quadratic formula$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
CircleArea $\pi r^2$, circumference $2\pi r$
TriangleArea $\frac{1}{2}bh$
CylinderVolume $\pi r^2 h$
Interior angles of a polygon$(n - 2) \cdot 180^\circ$
Tip Skip any question that will take more than two minutes, mark it, and return. Every question is worth the same.

What is the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon?

  1. $540^\circ$
  2. $720^\circ$
  3. $900^\circ$
  4. $1080^\circ$
Show answer

Answer: B. $(6 - 2) \times 180 = 720$.
