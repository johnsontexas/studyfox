Complex numbers

$i = \sqrt{-1}$, so $i^2 = -1$. Powers of $i$ repeat every 4: $i, -1, -i, 1$.

Example

$(3 + 2i)(1 - 4i) = 3 - 12i + 2i - 8i^2 = 3 - 10i + 8 = 11 - 10i$.

Tip To find $i^{n}$, divide $n$ by 4 and use the remainder: remainder 1 gives $i$, 2 gives $-1$, 3 gives $-i$, 0 gives $1$.

Matrices

  • Add or subtract matrices of the same size entry by entry.
  • To multiply, the number of columns in the first must equal the number of rows in the second. Each entry is a row times a column.
  • The determinant of $\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$ is $ad - bc$.
Example

$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ has determinant $1 \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot 3 = -2$.
