Functions and composition

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT Math · Lesson 4 of 6 · 7 min read

$f(g(x))$ means "plug $x$ into $g$, then plug the result into $f$." Work from the inside out.

Example

$f(x) = 2x + 1$ and $g(x) = x^2$. Find $f(g(3))$.

$g(3) = 9$, then $f(9) = 19$.

Incorrect$f(g(3)) = f(3) \cdot g(3)$. Composition is not multiplication.

Transformations

  • $f(x) + k$ shifts up $k$; $f(x - h)$ shifts right $h$.
  • $-f(x)$ reflects over the x-axis; $f(-x)$ reflects over the y-axis.
  • $a f(x)$ stretches vertically by a factor of $a$.
Tip "Right" goes with minus inside the parentheses. $f(x - 3)$ moves the graph 3 units to the right.

Check your understanding

If $f(x) = x - 4$ and $g(x) = 3x$, what is $g(f(6))$?

  1. 2
  2. 6
  3. 14
  4. 18
Show answer

Answer: B. $f(6) = 2$, then $g(2) = 6$.
