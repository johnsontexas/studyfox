Trigonometry beyond SOH CAH TOA

Open in StudyFox Free, no ads. Practice, track progress and quiz yourself in the app.

ACT Math · Lesson 5 of 6 · 8 min read

The ACT tests a little more trig than the SAT.

  • Identity: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$, and $\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
  • Unit circle: know the values at $0^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$, and the signs in each quadrant.
  • Graphs: in $y = a\sin(bx)$, the amplitude is $|a|$ and the period is $\frac{2\pi}{b}$.
  • Law of sines and cosines: the ACT usually gives these formulas in the question when they are needed.
Example

If $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$ and $\theta$ is in the first quadrant, then $\cos\theta = \frac{4}{5}$ (a 3-4-5 right triangle) and $\tan\theta = \frac{3}{4}$.

Check your understanding

What is the period of $y = 4\sin(2x)$?

  1. $\frac{\pi}{2}$
  2. $\pi$
  3. $2\pi$
  4. $4\pi$
Show answer

Answer: B. Period $= \frac{2\pi}{b} = \frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$. The 4 is the amplitude.
