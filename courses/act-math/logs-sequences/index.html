Logarithms

$\log_b x = y$ means $b^y = x$. A log answers the question "what power?"
  • $\log_2 8 = 3$ because $2^3 = 8$.
  • Product rule: $\log(ab) = \log a + \log b$. Quotient rule: $\log\frac{a}{b} = \log a - \log b$. Power rule: $\log a^n = n\log a$.
  • You can't take the log of zero or a negative number.

Sequences

TypePatternnth term
ArithmeticAdd the same number $d$$a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)d$
GeometricMultiply by the same number $r$$a_n = a_1 \cdot r^{n - 1}$
Example

The sequence 5, 11, 17, ... is arithmetic with $d = 6$. The 20th term is $5 + 19 \cdot 6 = 119$.
