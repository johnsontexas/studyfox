# Code of Conduct

StudyFox is a free study site for high school students. Everyone who takes part in this project, by opening issues, suggesting changes, or contributing code or content, is expected to help keep it a welcoming place.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with students and first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep content accurate, age-appropriate, and focused on learning.

Do not:

- Harass, insult, or demean anyone, including because of age, race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or background.
- Post sexual, violent, or hateful content.
- Share anyone's personal information without their permission.
- Spam, advertise, or try to monetize the project.

## Scope

This applies to the StudyFox repository, issues, pull requests, and any StudyFox community space.

## Reporting

Report problems to support@studyfox.org. Reports are kept private. The maintainer will review each report and may remove content, close discussions, or block people who break this code of conduct.
