The terms that show up most on the AP Bio exam, from cells to ecology.

AP · Biology · 25 cards

Hydrogen bond
Weak attraction between a slightly positive H and an electronegative atom; gives water cohesion and high specific heat
Dehydration synthesis
Joins monomers into polymers by removing a water molecule
Hydrolysis
Breaks polymers into monomers by adding water
Enzyme
Protein catalyst that lowers activation energy; shape of the active site sets specificity
Competitive inhibitor
Binds the active site and blocks substrate; overcome by adding more substrate
Allosteric regulation
A molecule binds away from the active site and changes enzyme shape and activity
Surface area-to-volume ratio
Shrinks as cells grow, which limits how big a cell can get
Active transport
Moving a substance against its gradient using ATP, e.g. the sodium-potassium pump
Water potential
Ψ = Ψs + Ψp; water moves from higher to lower water potential
Cellular respiration
Glycolysis → pyruvate oxidation → Krebs cycle → oxidative phosphorylation; makes ATP
Chemiosmosis
H⁺ flows down its gradient through ATP synthase, powering ATP production
Photosynthesis
Light reactions (thylakoid) make ATP and NADPH; Calvin cycle (stroma) fixes CO₂ into sugar
Signal transduction
Reception → transduction (often a phosphorylation cascade) → cellular response
Negative feedback
A response that reverses a change to keep conditions stable (homeostasis)
Cell cycle checkpoints
G1, G2, and M checkpoints; failures can lead to cancer
Meiosis
Two divisions making four haploid gametes; crossing over and independent assortment add variation
Nondisjunction
Chromosomes fail to separate, giving gametes with too many or too few chromosomes
Transcription
DNA → mRNA in the nucleus by RNA polymerase
Translation
mRNA → protein at the ribosome; tRNA anticodons pair with codons
Operon
Cluster of prokaryotic genes under one promoter, e.g. the inducible lac operon
Natural selection
Individuals with heritable traits better suited to the environment leave more offspring
Hardy-Weinberg
p² + 2pq + q² = 1; no evolution if no mutation, migration, selection, drift, and random mating
Genetic drift
Random change in allele frequencies, strongest in small populations (bottleneck, founder effect)
Logistic growth
Population growth slows as it nears carrying capacity K
Trophic levels
About 10% of energy passes from one level to the next
