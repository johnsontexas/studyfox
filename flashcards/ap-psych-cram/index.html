Key terms and researchers across AP Psych.

AP · Psychology · 20 cards

Neuron parts
Dendrites receive, axon sends, terminal buttons release neurotransmitters
Action potential
All-or-none electrical impulse down the axon
Dopamine
Neurotransmitter for reward and movement; linked to Parkinson's and schizophrenia
Serotonin
Neurotransmitter for mood, sleep, and appetite; low levels linked to depression
Frontal lobe
Planning, judgment, and personality; contains the motor cortex
Hippocampus
Forms new long-term memories
Amygdala
Processes fear and emotion
Classical conditioning
Learning by associating a neutral stimulus with an unconditioned stimulus (Pavlov)
Operant conditioning
Learning from consequences: reinforcement and punishment (Skinner)
Negative reinforcement
Removing something unpleasant to increase a behavior
Encoding, storage, retrieval
The three steps of memory
Serial position effect
Better recall of items at the start and end of a list
Confirmation bias
Seeking information that supports what you already believe
Piaget's stages
Sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete operational, formal operational
Maslow's hierarchy
Needs from physiological to safety, belonging, esteem, then self-actualization
Fundamental attribution error
Overestimating personality and underestimating the situation in others' behavior
Conformity (Asch)
People go along with a group even when it is clearly wrong
Obedience (Milgram)
People obey authority even when it means harming others
Big Five
Openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, neuroticism
Cognitive behavioral therapy
Changes harmful thought patterns and behaviors
