Slope of a line through (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂)
m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
Slope-intercept form
y = mx + b (m = slope, b = y-intercept)
Point-slope form
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
Parallel vs. perpendicular slopes
Parallel: equal slopes. Perpendicular: negative reciprocals (m₁·m₂ = −1)
Distance formula
d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
Midpoint formula
((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
Quadratic formula
x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a
Discriminant
b² − 4ac: >0 two real roots, =0 one, <0 none
Vertex of y = ax² + bx + c
x = −b / 2a
Vertex form of a parabola
y = a(x − h)² + k, vertex (h, k)
Sum and product of roots of ax² + bx + c
Sum = −b/a, Product = c/a
Difference of squares
a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)
Perfect square trinomial
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
Exponential growth/decay
y = a(1 ± r)ᵗ; r = rate as a decimal
Percent change
(new − old) / old × 100%
Exponent rules
xᵃ·xᵇ = xᵃ⁺ᵇ; xᵃ/xᵇ = xᵃ⁻ᵇ; (xᵃ)ᵇ = xᵃᵇ; x^(1/n) = ⁿ√x; x⁻ᵃ = 1/xᵃ
Circle equation
(x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², center (h, k), radius r
Arc length
(θ/360) × 2πr
Sector area
(θ/360) × πr²
Degrees ↔ radians
radians = degrees × π/180
SOH-CAH-TOA
sin = opp/hyp, cos = adj/hyp, tan = opp/adj
Complementary angle identity
sin θ = cos(90° − θ)
Pythagorean identity
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
30-60-90 triangle
Sides x : x√3 : 2x
45-45-90 triangle
Sides x : x : x√2
Common Pythagorean triples
3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25
Interior angle sum of an n-gon
(n − 2) × 180°
Similar figures scale factor k
Lengths ×k, areas ×k², volumes ×k³
Mean
Sum of values ÷ number of values
Probability
P = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes
Arithmetic sequence (ACT)
aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d; Sum = n(a₁ + aₙ)/2
Geometric sequence (ACT)
aₙ = a₁·rⁿ⁻¹
Logarithm definition (ACT)
log_b(x) = y ⟺ bʸ = x
Log rules (ACT)
log(xy) = log x + log y; log(x/y) = log x − log y; log(xⁿ) = n log x
Complex numbers (ACT)
i = √−1, i² = −1, i³ = −i, i⁴ = 1
2×2 determinant (ACT)
ad − bc
