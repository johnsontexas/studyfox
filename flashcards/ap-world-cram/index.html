AP World History Cram Set

Big empires, trade networks, and turning points from 1200 to now.

AP · World History · 16 cards

Song Dynasty
Chinese dynasty (960–1279) with Neo-Confucianism, champa rice, and a strong bureaucracy
Dar al-Islam
Lands under Islamic rule, linked by trade and scholarship
Mongol Empire
Largest land empire; protected Silk Road trade and spread the plague
Indian Ocean trade
Monsoon-driven sea trade linking East Africa, Arabia, India, and Southeast Asia
Mali Empire
West African empire grown rich from gold and salt trade; Mansa Musa
Gunpowder empires
Ottoman, Safavid, and Mughal states that expanded using firearms
Atlantic slave trade
Forced transport of millions of Africans to the Americas
Mercantilism
Economic policy of building national wealth through exports and colonies
Enlightenment
Ideas of reason, natural rights, and popular sovereignty that inspired revolutions
Industrial Revolution
Shift to machine production, starting in Britain about 1750
Imperialism
Expanding control over other territories, as in the Scramble for Africa
Meiji Restoration
Japan's rapid modernization and industrialization after 1868
Total war
Using a nation's entire economy and population for war, as in WWI and WWII
Decolonization
Colonies winning independence after WWII
Cold War
U.S.–Soviet rivalry from 1945 to 1991 fought through proxies and arms races
Globalization
Growing worldwide connections in trade, culture, and technology
