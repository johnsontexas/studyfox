Models, policies, and formulas for AP Macro.

AP · Economics · 16 cards

GDP
Market value of all final goods and services produced in a country in a year; C + I + G + Xn
Real vs. nominal GDP
Real GDP is adjusted for inflation
Unemployment rate
Unemployed ÷ labor force × 100
Types of unemployment
Frictional, structural, and cyclical; natural rate = frictional + structural
CPI
Price index of a typical basket of goods; used to measure inflation
Aggregate demand
Total spending at each price level; shifts with C, I, G, or Xn
Short-run aggregate supply
Shifts with input prices, productivity, and expectations
Recessionary gap
Output below full employment; fix with expansionary policy
Spending multiplier
1 ÷ MPS
Expansionary fiscal policy
Raise government spending or cut taxes
Money multiplier
1 ÷ reserve requirement
Open market operations
Fed buys bonds to increase the money supply, sells to decrease it
Federal funds rate
Rate banks charge each other for overnight loans; the Fed's main target
Phillips curve
Short-run trade-off between inflation and unemployment
Crowding out
Government borrowing raises interest rates and reduces private investment
Currency appreciation
A currency buys more foreign currency; exports fall, imports rise
