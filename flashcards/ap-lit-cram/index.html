Literary terms for poetry and prose analysis.

AP · English · 16 cards

Theme
The central insight about life a work conveys
Tone
The author's attitude toward the subject
Imagery
Language that appeals to the senses
Metaphor
Direct comparison without "like" or "as"
Symbol
An object that stands for a larger idea
Foil
A character who contrasts with another to highlight traits
Dramatic irony
The audience knows something a character does not
Unreliable narrator
A narrator whose account can't be fully trusted
Free indirect discourse
Third-person narration that slips into a character's thoughts
Enjambment
A sentence that runs past the end of a line of poetry
Caesura
A pause in the middle of a line of poetry
Iambic pentameter
Five pairs of unstressed-stressed syllables per line
Sonnet
14-line poem; Shakespearean ends with a couplet, Petrarchan turns at line 9
Volta
The turn or shift in a poem's argument or tone
Apostrophe
Addressing someone absent or something nonhuman
Bildungsroman
A coming-of-age novel
