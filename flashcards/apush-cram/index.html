Key events, laws, and ideas from 1491 to the present.

AP · U.S. History · 20 cards

Columbian Exchange
Transfer of crops, animals, people, and disease between the Americas and Europe/Africa
Encomienda system
Spanish grant of Native labor and tribute to colonists
Mayflower Compact
1620 Pilgrim agreement for self-government
Great Awakening
1730s–40s religious revival that challenged established churches
Salutary neglect
Britain's loose enforcement of colonial trade laws before 1763
Articles of Confederation
First U.S. government; weak central power, no taxing authority
Federalist Papers
Essays by Hamilton, Madison, and Jay urging ratification of the Constitution
Marbury v. Madison
1803 case establishing judicial review
Missouri Compromise
1820: Missouri slave, Maine free, no slavery north of 36°30′
Manifest Destiny
Belief the U.S. was destined to expand across North America
Dred Scott v. Sandford
1857: enslaved people were not citizens; Congress could not ban slavery in territories
Reconstruction Amendments
13th ended slavery, 14th citizenship and equal protection, 15th voting regardless of race
Gilded Age
Late 1800s era of industrial growth, monopolies, and political corruption
Progressive Era
Early 1900s reforms against corruption, monopolies, and unsafe conditions
New Deal
FDR's relief, recovery, and reform programs during the Great Depression
Containment
Cold War policy of stopping the spread of communism
Brown v. Board of Education
1954 ruling that school segregation is unconstitutional
Civil Rights Act of 1964
Banned discrimination in public places and employment
Great Society
LBJ's programs against poverty, including Medicare and Medicaid
Watergate
Scandal that led to Nixon's resignation in 1974
