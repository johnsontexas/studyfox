ACT Science Essentials

Experimental design and data vocabulary that shows up on every ACT Science section.

ACT · Science · 22 cards

Independent variable
The variable the experimenter deliberately changes
Dependent variable
The variable that is measured in response
Controlled variable (constant)
A factor kept the same in every trial
Control group
A group that does not receive the experimental treatment; used for comparison
Hypothesis
A testable prediction about the relationship between variables
Direct relationship
As one variable increases, the other increases
Inverse relationship
As one variable increases, the other decreases
Interpolation
Estimating a value between measured data points
Extrapolation
Estimating a value beyond the range of measured data
Trend
The overall pattern of change in data (increase, decrease, peak)
Mean
Average: sum divided by count
Trial
A single run of an experiment
Precision vs. accuracy
Precision = results agree with each other; accuracy = results are close to the true value
Density
Mass ÷ volume
Solubility
The maximum amount of a substance that dissolves in a solvent
pH
Scale of acidity: <7 acidic, 7 neutral, >7 basic
Catalyst / enzyme
Speeds up a reaction without being consumed
Boyle's law
At constant temperature, gas pressure and volume are inversely related
Pendulum period
Depends on length (and gravity), not on mass
Hooke's law
A spring stretches in proportion to the force applied
Conflicting viewpoints tip
Identify each scientist's main claim and the evidence each would accept
Reading graphs tip
Check axis labels, units, and the legend before reading any value
