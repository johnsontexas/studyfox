Cycles, ecosystems, and environmental laws for APES.

AP · Environmental Science · 15 cards

Biodiversity
Genetic, species, and ecosystem variety
Keystone species
A species with an outsized effect on its ecosystem
Carbon cycle
Carbon moves through photosynthesis, respiration, combustion, and ocean storage
Nitrogen fixation
Bacteria convert N₂ into ammonia that plants can use
Eutrophication
Excess nutrients cause algal blooms and low oxygen in water
10% rule
About 10% of energy moves up each trophic level
Primary succession
Ecosystem forms on bare rock with no soil, starting with pioneer species
Demographic transition
Countries move from high to low birth and death rates as they develop
Rule of 70
Doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate (%)
Tragedy of the commons
Shared resources get overused when individuals act in self-interest
Greenhouse gases
CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, and water vapor trap heat
Ozone depletion
CFCs break down stratospheric ozone that blocks UV radiation
Clean Air Act
U.S. law regulating air pollutants
Biomagnification
Toxins increase in concentration at higher trophic levels
Renewable energy
Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, and biomass
