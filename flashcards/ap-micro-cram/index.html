Market structures, graphs, and rules for AP Micro.

AP · Economics · 16 cards

Opportunity cost
The value of the next-best alternative given up
Comparative advantage
Producing a good at a lower opportunity cost than others
Law of demand
As price rises, quantity demanded falls
Price elasticity of demand
% change in quantity ÷ % change in price; > 1 is elastic
Price ceiling
Legal maximum price set below equilibrium; causes a shortage
Price floor
Legal minimum price set above equilibrium; causes a surplus
Deadweight loss
Lost total surplus from producing too much or too little
Marginal analysis
Keep doing something while marginal benefit ≥ marginal cost
Profit-maximizing rule
Produce where MR = MC
Shutdown rule
Shut down in the short run if price < minimum AVC
Perfect competition
Many firms, identical products, price takers; zero economic profit in the long run
Monopoly
One firm, price maker; produces less and charges more than a competitive market
Oligopoly
A few interdependent firms; analyzed with game theory
Nash equilibrium
No player can do better by changing strategy alone
Negative externality
A cost to third parties, e.g. pollution; market overproduces
Public good
Nonrival and nonexcludable, e.g. national defense
