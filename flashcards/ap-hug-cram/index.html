Models and vocabulary for AP Human Geography.

AP · Human Geography · 14 cards

Scale
The level of analysis: local, regional, national, or global
Population pyramid
Bar graph of a population by age and sex
Demographic Transition Model
Five stages of changing birth and death rates as a country develops
Push and pull factors
Reasons people leave a place and reasons they are drawn to another
Diffusion types
Relocation, hierarchical, contagious, and stimulus
Centripetal force
Something that unites a state, like a shared language
Gerrymandering
Drawing districts to favor one party
Von Thünen model
Agricultural land use rings around a market city
Green Revolution
High-yield seeds, fertilizer, and irrigation that raised food output
Central place theory
Settlements serve surrounding areas; larger ones offer more services
Burgess concentric zone model
City grows in rings out from the central business district
Rostow's stages of growth
Five stages from traditional society to high mass consumption
Wallerstein's world systems
Core, semi-periphery, and periphery countries
Sustainable development
Meeting present needs without harming future generations
