Rhetorical terms you need to analyze and argue.

AP · English · 16 cards

Rhetorical situation
Speaker, audience, purpose, exigence, and context
Exigence
The event or problem that prompts a piece of writing
Ethos
Appeal to the speaker's credibility and character
Pathos
Appeal to the audience's emotions
Logos
Appeal to logic and evidence
Claim
The arguable position a writer defends
Line of reasoning
The logical sequence of claims that supports the thesis
Concession
Acknowledging a valid point from the other side
Rebuttal
Responding to and countering an opposing argument
Anaphora
Repeating a word or phrase at the start of successive clauses
Antithesis
Placing contrasting ideas side by side in parallel structure
Diction
Word choice
Syntax
Sentence structure and arrangement
Juxtaposition
Placing two things close together to highlight contrast
Rhetorical question
A question asked for effect, not for an answer
Synthesis
Combining several sources to support your own argument
